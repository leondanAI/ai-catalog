Veredicto: qual escolher?

Escolha Beautiful.ai se…
  • ✅ You create presentations solo and want slides that auto-format as you add content
  • ✅ You prioritize speed — Beautiful.ai's Smart Slides eliminate manual layout work
  • ✅ You don't need real-time collaboration or team analytics
  • ✅ Your budget is tight — Beautiful.ai Pro is cheaper than Pitch's team plans
Escolha Pitch se…
  • ✅ Your team creates presentations together and needs real-time co-editing
  • ✅ You want analytics — Pitch shows who viewed your deck and for how long
  • ✅ You pitch investors or clients and need professional templates with brand control
  • ✅ You want built-in video comments and async collaboration on slides

Perguntas frequentes

What is Beautiful.ai used for?
Beautiful.ai is an AI presentation tool where slides auto-format as you add content. It's designed for individuals who create business presentations and want professional-looking results without manual design work.
Does Pitch have AI features?
Yes. Pitch includes AI slide generation, AI content suggestions, and AI-powered design assistance. It also offers real-time collaboration, viewer analytics, and team workspaces — differentiating it from solo-focused tools.
Is Beautiful.ai free?
Beautiful.ai offers a free plan with limited slides and features. The Pro plan starts at $12/month and the Team plan at $40/month per user, providing unlimited slides and collaboration.
Which is better for investor pitches — Beautiful.ai or Pitch?
Pitch is generally preferred for investor pitches due to its professional templates, viewer analytics (see when investors open your deck), and team collaboration. Beautiful.ai works well for internal business presentations.
