Best AI for presentations

Pick Gamma to turn a quick outline into a finished deck fast, Canva AI if you want on-brand visual design across formats, and Pitch if your team needs real-time collaboration on branded decks.

Fastest from scratch

Gamma

Free plan available👥 100M+

Gamma turns a short prompt or outline into a full deck with structure, writing, and layout already done. You describe the topic, then refine the text and images on the page or switch to the freeform canvas for manual control. Heavy use can burn through the credit allowance quickly, so check how far your plan stretches before relying on it daily.

Best for on-brand visuals

Canva AI

Free plan available👥 220M+

Canva AI sits inside the Canva editor, so a generated deck lives alongside your other brand assets like social posts and documents. You describe what you want and the assistant drafts editable slides you can tweak in the same tool. It handles common slide formats well but can struggle with highly custom layouts.

Canva AI 2.0 is the conversational core of Canva's Magic Studio, introduced at Canva Create 2026: you describe a design in text or voice and it returns an editable layout, built on Canva's own Design Model.

Best for team collaboration

Pitch

Free plan available👥 4M+

Pitch Agent drafts a full presentation from a prompt and any files you upload, built from your team's approved templates so it stays on-brand. Teammates can comment and edit the same deck live, and you can see who opens a shared link. It only handles presentations, so you'll need another tool for documents or spreadsheets.

Pitch is an AI presentation workspace built around Pitch Agent, a conversational AI that generates fully on-brand decks from a prompt and your files, then rewrites, splits, and refines slides on request.

How to do it with AI

  1. Write a short outline or paste your notes so the AI has something to work from.
  2. Pick a tool and let it generate a first draft of the full deck.
  3. Go through each slide and tighten the wording, swap images, and fix awkward layouts.
  4. Check the draft for factual mistakes, wrong numbers, and off-brand colors or fonts before sharing it.
  5. Export or share the deck in the format your audience needs, like a PDF or a live link.

FAQ

Can I do this with AI for free?
Yes: Gamma, Canva AI and Pitch offer a free plan. Free plans usually come with usage limits; details are on each tool page.
Can AI write the content of my slides, not just the design?
Yes. Gamma, Canva AI, and Pitch can all draft slide text from a prompt or outline, but you should still read it over and edit it so it sounds like you and is accurate.
Will an AI-made deck look generic?
It depends on the tool and how much you guide it. Giving a clear outline, your own images, and your brand colors helps the result look less like a template and more like your own deck.
