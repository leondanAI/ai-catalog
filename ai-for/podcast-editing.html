Best AI to edit podcasts and add subtitles

If you want to edit by rewriting a transcript and let AI handle cleanup and clips, use Descript; if your main problem is noisy or echoey audio, run it through Adobe Podcast first; and if you need quick captions and a polished cut for social platforms, finish in CapCut.
Picked by AItoolFit · tool data checked 2026-10-18
1
Transcript-based editing and cleanup

Descript

Free plan available👥 6M+

Descript turns your podcast recording into a text transcript, so you cut and rearrange the conversation by editing words instead of dragging audio on a timeline. It can also remove filler words, reduce noise, and generate subtitles and show notes from the same transcript. The AI features run on a metered credit system, so heavy use can mean buying more credits.

Descript is a transcript-based audio and video editor whose current centerpiece is Underlord (2026), a generative AI co-editor that drafts scripts, writes show notes, finds shareable clips, and generates and extends video.

2
Clean up noisy recordings first

Adobe Podcast

Free plan available

Adobe Podcast focuses on making voice recordings sound clear, stripping background noise and room echo so the episode sounds like it was recorded in a quiet studio. It also handles multitrack recordings from several speakers, syncing them automatically. Deep editing and subtitles aren't its focus, so people often pair it with another tool afterward.

Adobe Podcast is a browser-based audio tool whose flagship feature, Enhance Speech, cleans up spoken recordings to sound studio-quality.

Open Adobe Podcast → Adobe Podcast review
3
Fast captions and social-ready export

CapCut

Free plan available👥 800M+

CapCut adds automatic captions to your podcast video and gives you a full timeline to trim, arrange and polish the episode for sharing. Templates, transitions and stock audio help turn a long recording into a short clip quickly. The deeper AI tools and higher-resolution export sit behind a paid tier, so the free version can feel limited for longer episodes.

CapCut is a cross-platform video editor for mobile, desktop and web.

What to choose

  • 🎙️You want to cut and edit your episode by changing the text of its transcript → Descript
  • 🧹Your recording has background noise or echo that needs cleaning up first → Adobe Podcast
  • 📱You want to turn the episode into a short captioned video for social media → CapCut
  • 📝You just need a plain transcript of the recording, nothing else → Otter.ai

How to do it with AI

  1. Record or import your podcast audio and video into an editor.
  2. Clean up the sound, removing background noise, echo and long pauses.
  3. Edit the episode by trimming, reordering or cutting sections, using the transcript to speed this up.
  4. Generate captions or subtitles automatically, then read them against the actual audio to fix misheard words and timing.
  5. Export the finished episode and check it plays back correctly on the platform you'll publish it to.

FAQ

Can I do this with AI for free?
Yes: Descript, Adobe Podcast and CapCut offer a free plan. Free plans usually come with usage limits; details are on each tool page.
Can I edit a podcast without touching a timeline?
Yes. Tools like Descript let you cut and rearrange the recording by editing a text transcript, and the audio and video follow your changes automatically.
Do automatic subtitles need to be checked afterward?
Yes. Automatic captions can mishear names, technical terms and background noise, so it's worth reading them against the audio before you publish.
