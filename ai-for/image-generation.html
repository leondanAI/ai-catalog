Best AI to generate images

Use Nano Banana for fast, free image editing inside Google's chat app, ChatGPT Images if you already work in ChatGPT and need accurate text or UI in your picture, and Midjourney when the artistic quality of the final image matters most.
Picked by AItoolFit · tool data checked 2026-10-11
1
Free conversational editing

Nano Banana

Free plan available

You describe the picture you want in plain language inside Google's chat app, then keep talking to it to refine the result over several turns. It's good at keeping the same character or subject consistent as you make changes. The catch is that free images carry a visible watermark on top of the usual invisible one.

Nano Banana is Google's family of Gemini image generation and editing models, including Nano Banana 2, Nano Banana 2 Lite, Nano Banana 2.1 and Nano Banana Pro, available through the Gemini app, Google AI Studio and the Gemini API.

What to choose

  • 💬You want to generate and edit images for free inside a chat app → Nano Banana
  • ✍️You need an image with readable text, icons or a busy layout → ChatGPT Images
  • 🎨You want the richest, most polished artwork for a portfolio or print → Midjourney
  • 🖼️You want to try many different AI art styles in one place → Magnific (formerly Freepik)

How to do it with AI

  1. Write a clear description of the picture you want, including subject, style and setting.
  2. Pick a tool that matches your need: fast free edits, in-chat convenience, or top art quality.
  3. Generate a first version and look closely at faces, hands, text and small details.
  4. Refine your wording or ask for a specific edit to fix anything that looks wrong.
  5. Download the final image once you're happy with the result.

FAQ

Can I do this with AI for free?
Yes: Nano Banana and ChatGPT Images offer a free plan. Midjourney is paid only. Free plans usually come with usage limits; details are on each tool page.
Can I edit a photo I already have instead of starting from nothing?
Yes. Nano Banana and ChatGPT Images both accept an existing image alongside your text instructions, so you can describe the change you want instead of generating a brand new picture.
Why do faces or hands sometimes look wrong in AI images?
Image models can still struggle with fine detail like hands, text and small objects. Check these areas closely and ask the tool to redo or fix just that part if something looks off.
