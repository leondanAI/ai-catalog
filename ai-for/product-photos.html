Best AI for product photos

Use Photoroom for clean white-background shots and batch catalog editing, Krea AI when you want creative lifestyle scenes built around the product, and Canva AI to turn those photos into on-brand listing and marketing layouts.

Built for product photos

Photoroom

Free plan available👥 80M+

Photoroom removes backgrounds, adds realistic shadows and places your product into AI-made scenes from one upload. You can batch-process a whole catalog at once and connect it to your store, which saves hours on repetitive listing photos. Its free option is for personal use only, so a store will need a paid plan to export in bulk.

Photoroom is an AI photo editor for product photography that removes backgrounds, adds shadows and AI-generated scenes, and batch-edits catalog images on mobile, web and through an API.

Best for lifestyle scenes

Krea AI

Free plan available👥 Free / from $9/mo

Krea AI generates full lifestyle backgrounds and styles around your product, with sliders you can nudge until the mood feels right. You upload your product shot, describe or steer the scene, and iterate quickly across many looks. Heavy use of video or high-resolution output can use up your allowance fast, so it suits occasional creative scenes more than daily bulk editing.

Krea AI is a web-based creative suite for AI image generation, video generation, 3D generation, and AI upscaling, built around Krea 3, the company's in-house image model that became the default in the app on October 16, 2026, with a Krea 3 Ultra variant on Max plans and above.

Easiest for catalog layouts

Canva AI

Free plan available👥 220M+

Canva AI takes a finished product photo and helps you build the material around it, like social posts, listing banners and catalog pages, just by describing what you want. It keeps AI-generated designs editable instead of flattening them, so you can adjust text or colors later. It's better for assembling and presenting photos than for generating the original white-background shot itself.

Canva AI 2.0 is the conversational core of Canva's Magic Studio, introduced at Canva Create 2026: you describe a design in text or voice and it returns an editable layout, built on Canva's own Design Model.

How to do it with AI

  1. Shoot or gather your raw product photos with decent lighting and a plain backdrop if you can.
  2. Use an AI tool to remove the background and drop in a clean white or custom backdrop.
  3. Generate a lifestyle or styled scene around the product if you want context beyond a plain background.
  4. Batch-process the rest of your catalog once you're happy with one result, so every photo matches.
  5. Check the result for warped edges, odd shadows or wrong colors before publishing, and fix by hand if needed.

FAQ

Can I do this with AI for free?
Yes: Photoroom, Krea AI and Canva AI offer a free plan. Free plans usually come with usage limits; details are on each tool page.
Can AI create product photos without a real photo shoot?
Not entirely. Most tools still need a real photo of your product to work from; they remove the background, add shadows and place it in a new scene. Products invented fully by AI with no source photo tend to look less accurate.
Will AI-edited product photos look consistent across my whole catalog?
They can, if you use the same tool and settings for every image and run them as a batch rather than one by one. Mixing tools or redoing settings for each photo is the main reason catalogs end up looking inconsistent.
