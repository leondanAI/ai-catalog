How to do it with AI

  1. Pick a topic or keyword your audience actually searches for.
  2. Research what's already ranking and what AI answers currently cite.
  3. Draft or optimize your page using the tool's scoring or editor.
  4. Publish the page and check how it performs in both Google rankings and AI visibility reports.
  5. Revisit and update the page over time as rankings and AI mentions shift.

FAQ

Can I do this with AI for free?
Yes: Semrush offers a free plan. Surfer SEO and Frase are paid only. Free plans usually come with usage limits; details are on each tool page.
Do I still need to write for Google if AI answers are taking over search?
Yes. AI answer engines pull from pages that already rank well and look trustworthy, so solid SEO still comes first.
How is optimizing for AI answers different from normal SEO?
Normal SEO targets ranking pages for keywords. AI-answer optimization also looks at whether your content gets cited inside tools like ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews, which depends on clarity, structure and trust signals.
