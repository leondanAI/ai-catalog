Best AI for voiceovers

Use ElevenLabs for the most natural, multilingual voice across narration, promo and explainer videos, Murf AI if you want a built-in editor to sync voice with slides or video, and Speechify if you mainly need to turn a written script or document into spoken audio fast.
Picked by AItoolFit · tool data checked 2026-10-25
1
Most natural multilingual voice

ElevenLabs

Free plan available

ElevenLabs turns a script into lifelike speech in many languages, and you can clone a voice if you want a consistent narrator across videos. You type or paste your script, pick a voice, generate the audio, then drop it into your video editor. Usage is credit-based, so long-form projects can use up your allowance faster than you'd expect.

ElevenLabs is a multimodal AI studio on a single credit balance: speech, voice cloning, music, sound effects, transcription, dubbing, and image and video generation with lip sync.

Voice synced to your video

Murf AI

Free plan available👥 10M+

Murf AI gives you a studio editor where you can line up a voiceover with your slides or video timeline, and adjust pacing, emphasis and tone. It's built with explainer and training content in mind, so you can fine-tune timing without leaving the app. The free plan won't let you download the audio or use it commercially, so it's mainly for testing.

AI voice platform built around a studio-style voiceover editor (200+ voices, 30+ languages and accents) for e-learning, explainer videos, and presentations, alongside a separate conversational voice-agent product and a low-latency text-to-speech API (Falcon).

3
Script to speech, fast

Speechify

Free plan available👥 20M+

Speechify is built to turn written text into spoken audio quickly, which makes it handy for turning a script or document straight into a voiceover track. You paste in your text, choose a voice, and export the audio to use in your video. The best voices and faster playback speeds sit behind the paid plan.

Speechify is a text-to-speech and voice-AI platform built on its proprietary SIMBA 3.x voice models.

How to do it with AI

  1. Write or finalize the script you want narrated.
  2. Pick a voice that matches the tone of your video.
  3. Generate the voiceover and adjust pacing, emphasis or pronunciation where needed.
  4. Sync the audio to your video timeline and check the levels match your visuals.
  5. Listen back to the full video and fix any mispronounced words or awkward pauses before exporting.

FAQ

Can I do this with AI for free?
Yes: ElevenLabs, Murf AI and Speechify offer a free plan. Free plans usually come with usage limits; details are on each tool page.
Will the AI voice sound robotic?
Modern AI voices like these are designed to sound natural, with realistic pacing and intonation. Quality varies by voice, so try a few options and pick the one that fits your script's tone.
Can I use these voices for commercial projects?
Most of these tools allow commercial use of generated voiceovers on paid plans, but free plans often restrict commercial use, so check the specific plan's terms before publishing your video.
