Best AI to generate videos

Pick Kling AI for polished short clips you build shot by shot, Google Veo when you need audio generated along with the video, and Higgsfield if you want to test several video models, including both, inside one ad-focused workspace.
Picked by AItoolFit · tool data checked 2026-10-30
1
Cinematic clips, shot by shot

Kling AI

Paid only

Kling AI turns a text description or a photo into a short, polished video clip. You write the scene, add reference images if you want the same character or product to appear in multiple shots, and Kling generates the clip with its own audio track. It works best when you're happy to build a video shot by shot rather than get one long continuous take.

Kling AI is a text- and image-to-video generator from Kuaishou.

Short clips with built-in audio

Google Veo

Paid only

Google Veo creates short clips from a text prompt or a single photo, and generates matching audio, like dialogue, effects and ambience, in the same step, so you don't add sound separately. It lives inside Google Flow and the Gemini app, and developers can call it through an API with clear per-use pricing. The main trade-off is that each clip is short, so longer ads need several generations stitched together.

Google's text- and image-to-video model, currently shipping as Veo 3.1, generates short clips with synchronized dialogue, sound effects and ambient audio.

3
One subscription, many video models

Higgsfield

Free plan available👥 40M+

Higgsfield is a workspace where you pick a model, including Kling and Google Veo, for each job, rather than subscribing to each one separately. It adds ready-made tools for ads, product placement and AI presenters, so you can go from a brief to a finished ad without leaving the platform. Costs run on credits that vary by model and resolution, so it takes a bit of planning to keep a budget steady.

Higgsfield is an AI creative platform that bundles third-party video and image models, such as Kling, Seedance, Google Veo and Nano Banana, with its own tools for ads, social clips and AI influencers.

  • 🔊You need the clip to come with matching sound already built in → Google Veo

How to do it with AI

  1. Write a short, clear description of the scene, or pick a photo to animate.
  2. Choose a tool based on whether you need audio built in, multiple shots with the same character, or several models in one place.
  3. Generate a first clip and review pacing, motion and any visual glitches.
  4. Adjust your prompt or reference images and regenerate until the result matches your brief.
  5. Export the clip and check it on the platform you'll actually publish to, since cropping and sound can look different there.

FAQ

Can I do this with AI for free?
Yes: Higgsfield offers a free plan. Kling AI and Google Veo are paid only. Free plans usually come with usage limits; details are on each tool page.
Can I turn a single photo into a moving video?
Yes. Kling AI, Google Veo and Higgsfield all support image-to-video, so you upload a still photo and the tool animates it into a short clip based on your prompt.
Will the clip come with sound, or do I need to add it separately?
Google Veo generates audio in the same step as the video. Kling AI also produces audio with its clips. On Higgsfield, whether you get built-in audio depends on which underlying model you pick for the job.
