Best AI to turn blog posts into videos

Use Synthesia or HeyGen to turn a script into an avatar-led video quickly, then use CapCut to cut, caption and resize it for social feeds.

Script to avatar video fast

Synthesia

Free plan available👥 50K+

Paste your blog text as a script, pick an avatar and voice, and Synthesia renders a presenter-style video with no camera or studio needed. It works well for explainer-style social clips and can localize the same video into other languages. Videos are capped by monthly minutes, so a long post may need trimming first.

How to do it with AI

  1. Pick the key points from your blog post and rewrite them as a short script, one idea per sentence.
  2. Choose an avatar and voice, or record and upload footage, then generate a first video draft.
  3. Trim the draft down to the length your platform favors and add captions.
  4. Export in the right format, vertical for Reels and TikTok, square or horizontal for other feeds.
  5. Watch the full video back and check the pacing, captions and pronunciation before posting.

FAQ

Can I do this with AI for free?
Yes: Synthesia, HeyGen and CapCut offer a free plan. Free plans usually come with usage limits; details are on each tool page.
Do I need to film anything myself?
No. Synthesia and HeyGen generate the video from an avatar and your script, so no camera is required. CapCut is for editing footage you already have, including avatar-generated video.
How long should a blog-to-video clip be for social media?
Most social platforms favor short clips, often under a minute. Shorten your script to the main point and let the tool trim or cut the rest.
