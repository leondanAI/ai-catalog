Best AI to make a logo

If you want a fast, affordable logo with ready-made brand assets, start with Looka; if you already design in Canva AI and want your logo to match other marketing materials, build it there; if you need true vector files and more control over style, use Recraft.
Picked by AItoolFit · tool data checked 2026-10-30
1
Fast branded logo kit

Looka

Free plan available👥 2M+

Looka asks about your company name, industry and style, then generates a set of logo concepts you can tweak in its editor — fonts, colors, layout and icons. Once you pick a direction, its Brand Kit turns the logo into matching social posts, cards and other marketing assets. The catch is you can't download a usable file until you pay, so test a few directions before committing.

Looka is an AI logo maker and branding platform.

2
Logo inside your design kit

Canva AI

Free plan available👥 220M+

Canva AI lets you describe a logo in plain language and get back an editable design inside the same editor you use for social posts, slides and other marketing materials. This is useful if you want the logo to match everything else you make for the business from day one. It works best for clean, simple marks rather than highly custom illustration.

Canva AI 2.0 is the conversational core of Canva's Magic Studio, introduced at Canva Create 2026: you describe a design in text or voice and it returns an editable layout, built on Canva's own Design Model.

3
True vector logo files

Recraft

Free plan available👥 3M+

Recraft generates both raster images and genuine editable vector files, which matters for a logo you'll resize for signs, packaging and print. You write a prompt, pick a style, and refine the result in its Studio using strong style controls to keep variations consistent. It has a steeper learning curve than the other two, so it suits people comfortable with a design tool.

Recraft AI is a design-focused image generator built around its Recraft V4.1 model family, including a fast Flash variant.

What to choose

  • 🚀You want several logo options fast and don't know where to start → Looka
  • 🎨You already use Canva for your flyers, posts and slides → Canva AI
  • 🖼️You need a true vector file for print or signage → Recraft
  • ✍️You want text worked neatly into the logo image itself → Ideogram

How to do it with AI

  1. Write down your business name, industry and three words that describe your style, like modern, playful or classic.
  2. Generate several logo concepts in your chosen tool and save the ones you like.
  3. Refine your favorite by adjusting colors, fonts, icon and layout until it feels right.
  4. Check the logo at a small size, like a favicon, and on a dark background to make sure it still reads clearly.
  5. Download the file formats you need — vector for print, PNG for web — and save your brand colors and fonts for later use.

FAQ

Can I do this with AI for free?
Yes: Looka, Canva AI and Recraft offer a free plan. Free plans usually come with usage limits; details are on each tool page.
Do I own the logo an AI tool creates for me?
Usually yes, once you've downloaded a paid file — check each tool's terms, since some mark free or public designs as not licensed for commercial use.
Will an AI-made logo look like everyone else's?
It can, if you accept the first result. Generate several options, then customize colors, fonts and icons so the final logo feels specific to your business.
