## Основное

- **Название:** AItoolFit (не «AI ToolFit»; везде одинаково)
- **Адрес:** https://aitoolfit.ai
- **Логотип:** `favicon-192.png` (квадрат 192×192), для 240×240 — `favicon.svg`, экспортировать в нужный размер
- **Картинка для соцсетей / обложка:** `og-image.png`
- **Скриншоты:** `media/1.png` … `media/5.png` (порядок 1 → 5). Сделаны в мае: если площадка требует свежие, сказать — пересниму.
- **Цена:** бесплатно, без регистрации
- **Языки:** EN, ES, DE, RU, UA, HE, FR, PT (8)
- **Категории:** AI Search / Recommendation engine, Productivity. Не «AI directory» — там мы конкурент, а не продукт.

## Тексты (английский)

**Слоган, до 60 знаков**
> Describe your task, get 3 AI tools that fit.

**Короткое описание, до 160 знаков**
> Free AI tool finder: describe any task in plain words and get 3 matched AI tools with reasons why each fits. No signup, 8 languages.

**Описание, ~500 знаков**
> AItoolFit is a free, task-based AI tool finder. Instead of scrolling through directories with thousands of listings, you describe what you need to get done ("turn a podcast into show notes", "build a landing page without code") and get 3 AI tools that fit that exact task, each with a short explanation of why. Behind it: 120 hand-checked tool cards with current versions and prices, side-by-side comparisons, and weekly AI news. No signup. Available in 8 languages.

**Длинное описание, ~1200 знаков** (для форм, где можно больше)
> There are tens of thousands of AI tools and new ones every day. Picking the right one for a specific job usually means hours of sign-ups, tutorials and dead ends.
>
> AItoolFit works the other way round. You describe your task in plain language and get 3 AI tools that actually match it, with a clear reason for each pick. Recommendations are ranked by fit, never by payment.
>
> Every tool in the catalog has a card that is re-checked against the vendor's own site: current model versions, real prices and limits, pros and cons, and "choose it if…" advice. There are side-by-side comparisons (Claude vs ChatGPT, Cursor vs GitHub Copilot and dozens more) and a weekly AI news section, including surveys of which tools professionals actually use.
>
> Free, no account needed, available in English, Spanish, German, Russian, Ukrainian, Hebrew, French and Portuguese.

**Для Hacker News / Reddit (одна строка)**
> Show HN: AItoolFit – describe a task, get 3 AI tools that fit (free, no signup)
