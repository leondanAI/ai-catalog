## Long Description (for forms that allow 1000–2000 chars)

There are tens of thousands of AI tools — and hundreds more launched every day. Finding the right one for a specific task wastes hours: wrong registrations, tutorials for tools that don't fit, starting over.

AI ToolFit solves this differently. You describe your task in plain language — "redesign game UI using AI," "write a cold email campaign," "transcribe podcast and summarize" — and get 3 curated tools that actually match, with a clear explanation of why each one works for that exact job.

It's not a catalog. It's not a list sorted by popularity. It's a recommendation engine built around what you're trying to do, not what's trending.

**How it works:**
1. Describe your task (takes 10 seconds)
2. Get 3 best-fit tools with explanations
3. Compare and choose — save hours of searching

**Who it's for:** Founders, marketers, designers, developers, creators, students — anyone who uses AI tools and wastes time finding the right one.

**Available in 6 languages:** English, Spanish, German, Russian, Ukrainian, Hebrew.

Free to use. No registration required.
