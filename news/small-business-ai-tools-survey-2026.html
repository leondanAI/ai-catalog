Small Businesses Pick ChatGPT (57%) and Gemini (56%), Claude Is at 12%: Bluevine's 2026 AI Survey

Research Nov 15, 2026·Bluevine

Bluevine, a US business banking platform for small companies, published its 2026 small business AI report in July. The survey was run by Centiment on April 7–9, 2026, among 942 US small business owners and financial decision-makers at companies with 2 to 249 employees and annual revenue between $50,000 and $5 million. Bluevine puts the margin of error at about ±3%.

Which tools they use

ChatGPT is used by 57% of respondents and Gemini by 56%, with Microsoft Copilot third at 30%. Further behind are Claude (12%), Canva AI (11%) and Perplexity (9%). That is a very different picture from our survey of marketers, where Claude almost matched ChatGPT.

74% of owners already use or are testing AI tools. The most common uses are data analysis and business insights (39%, up from 33% last year), marketing and sales (37%), automating operations (29%), customer service (27%) and financial management (23%).

Bluevine also looked at payments made by its own customers. This is not survey data, and Bluevine notes it does not represent small businesses broadly. More of its customers use ChatGPT than any other AI app, but Claude users grew 729% year over year, and by April 2026 Claude had overtaken ChatGPT in total monthly transactions. Among coding tools, Cursor is up 162%.

Time saved, spending and barriers

Almost half of AI users (48%) save at least 4 hours a week: 23% save 4–6 hours, 11% save 7–9 and 14% save 10 or more. 52% report a return on their investment, while 24% have not seen one yet.

Spending is modest. A third of owners (33%) pay nothing for AI tools, 28% spend $25–99 a month, 16% spend $100–249 and only 10% spend $250 or more. Bluevine suggests the free tiers of ChatGPT and Gemini are part of what drives adoption.

82% have hit at least one barrier. The top two are data security and privacy concerns (33%, up from 23% in 2025) and distrust of AI's accuracy (31%).

What this means if you run a small business

Start with the free tiers. Most owners in the survey use ChatGPT, Gemini or Microsoft Copilot, and a third pay nothing. Run the same real task through two of them before you pay for anything; our Gemini vs ChatGPT comparison covers the main differences.

Match the tool to the job. For data analysis, upload a real export from your accounting or sales system and check a few figures by hand. For marketing, compare drafts side by side; Canva AI makes sense if you already design in Canva. For operations, look first at the assistant built into software you already use, such as Gemini in Google Workspace or Copilot in Microsoft 365: Bluevine says that when owners pay a premium, it is usually for AI built into tools they use every day. More options are in our list of the best AI chatbots and assistants.

Check data handling before you upload anything. Before pasting customer lists or financial records, read how the plan stores your data, whether it is used to train models, and whether you can switch that off.

Keep a person in the loop. With 31% distrusting AI's accuracy, review numbers, invoices and customer replies before they go out.
