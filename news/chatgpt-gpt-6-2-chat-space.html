GPT-6.2 Reaches ChatGPT's Chat Tab, and Space for Shared Pages Opens to Plus

Tools Nov 23, 2026·OpenAI Help Center

A week after GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol arrived in the API, OpenAI is bringing the new model into the main chat of ChatGPT. The rollout started on November 23 for Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise; on Enterprise, availability depends on admin settings.

What changes in the chat

The chat tab now runs on a version of GPT-6.2 Sol tuned for everyday conversation. OpenAI says it handles complex requests with detailed instructions or web searches better, for example weighing price against location when comparing hotels, and knows when to follow up: if you ask whether something is on sale, ChatGPT can offer to tell you when the price drops.

OpenAI's own numbers, compared with GPT-6 in the chat tab:

Image understanding: 71.7%, up from 47.4%.
Following instructions on especially hard prompts: 78.1%, up from 62.5%.
Internal expert-level tasks at the lowest reasoning setting: 65%, up from 52%.
Web research (internal version of BrowseComp) at Extra high reasoning: 64.5% versus 54.4%.

On safety, OpenAI reports responses met its policies in 91–94% of challenging conversations, up from 77–80% for GPT-6, with the largest gains on teen safety topics. The models behind the Work and Codex tabs are not changing in this release.

Space opens to Plus

On November 20, ChatGPT Space began rolling out globally to Plus users aged 18 and over. In Space you create interactive pages and presentations with ChatGPT and invite other people with Space access to work on them. Pages and slides can update themselves from your connected apps: OpenAI's examples are a to-do list that keeps itself current, a study guide with a 3D model, and a project plan that follows decisions made in Slack. You can tag ChatGPT in a comment to ask for changes.

Space replaces the Library in the sidebar and becomes the home for pages, slides, images and uploaded files. It works on the web, in the desktop app and on iOS and Android. Sharing has limits: viewers need a Plus, Pro, Business or Enterprise account with Space access, Free users can't open shared pages, and there are no public links.

OpenAI also started rolling out a redesigned mobile app on November 19, beginning with Pro users aged 18+ outside the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and Korea, and a small group of Plus users.

Who it matters to

For paying subscribers the update is automatic: everyday chats get the newer model without picking anything. Space is OpenAI's answer to document-style collaboration that tools like Notion AI and Gamma already offer; the catch for teams is that everyone involved needs a paid plan. Comparing assistants?
