Gemini 4 Hydrogen Is Out: Google's Fast Model Reaches Search, Free Gemini Users and the API at $1.50/$7.50

Models Nov 12, 2026·Google Blog

Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen on November 12, a fast, efficient model for everyday coding and agent work that Google calls its "daily driver for agentic workflows." It reaches ordinary users on day one: it now runs in Google Search for everyone and is available in the Gemini app, including on the free plan. Two days earlier, Google also opened Gemini 4 Carbon, its strongest model, to paying developers and AI Ultra subscribers.

What Hydrogen is

Hydrogen is built for quick execution, while the frontier models Carbon and Argon are meant for deep, long-running reasoning. It accepts up to 1 million tokens of input and has four thinking levels: low, medium, high (the default) and a new xhigh. By Google's own figures, Hydrogen at the default level scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, one point above Argon, and finishes tasks 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash.

Where you can use it

Google Search: Hydrogen is available globally to all users, in every language that AI Mode supports. Google describes it as its largest model upgrade for Search so far: it handles longer multi-part questions and shows more comparison tables, images and links to a wider range of sites.

Gemini app: Hydrogen is in the model menu. Free users get it through automatic model selection when it suits the prompt; Google notes that most prompts still go to the lighter Flash-Lite for quick answers.

Developers: in the API, Hydrogen costs $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens, the same across the full 1M-token context. It is also in Google AI Studio, Android Studio and, for AI Pro and Ultra subscribers, Google Antigravity.

GitHub Copilot: since November 13, Hydrogen is generally available in GitHub Copilot on the Pro, Pro+, Max, Business and Enterprise plans. The rollout is gradual, with usage-based billing at Google's list price.

Carbon opens up, with a price deadline

Since November 10, Gemini 4 Carbon is available to all paying developers through the paid API tier and Gemini for business, and is rolling out to Google AI Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app, Antigravity and AI Mode in Search. The introductory price is $2/$10 per million tokens until November 30, then $4/$20. Google itself says Argon remains the right choice for most software and knowledge work, and that Carbon uses more tokens than Argon.

What this means if you are choosing an AI assistant

Free users already have the new model. If you rely on Google Search or free Gemini, Hydrogen now handles part of your requests without any action on your side. Before paying, retry your real tasks on the free tiers; our Gemini vs ChatGPT comparison shows where the two still differ.

Check Carbon costs before December. If you are testing Carbon in the API, budget for double the price from December 1, and compare its results with Argon, which Google recommends for most work.

Hydrogen is the cheap default for coding agents. For high-volume coding and agent tasks it costs less per token than Carbon, even at Carbon's introductory price. For other options, see our list of AI tools for coding.
