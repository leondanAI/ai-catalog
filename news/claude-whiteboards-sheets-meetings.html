Claude Adds Whiteboards, Sheets and Data, and Starts Joining Google Meet Calls

Tools Nov 10, 2026·Anthropic

Anthropic is pushing Claude further into office-suite territory. On November 10 it launched Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets in beta and made Claude Dashboards generally available under a new name, Claude Data. Two days later it began rolling out Claude Meetings in beta, which lets Claude join a Google Meet call as a visible participant.

What's new

Whiteboards is a shared board where a team brainstorms with Claude. You can talk through a rough plan and Claude turns it into a board with a timeline, owners and dates. In a retrospective, for example, everyone adds notes, and Claude groups them by theme and drafts action items. You can drag a box around part of the board and ask Claude about it. A finished board can become a doc or a deck, or move to Lucid, Miro or Vani by Zoho.

Sheets builds spreadsheets with tabs, formulas, formatting and drop-downs, often from a single prompt such as a hiring budget. Claude edits directly in the cells and outlines each edit so you can track changes, and sheets can be shared for joint work. Anthropic says more features are coming. For full Excel features it points you to .xlsx or CSV export, or to Claude for Excel.

Claude Data, formerly Dashboards, launched in beta in October and answers questions about company data in tools like Amazon Redshift, BigQuery and ClickHouse. It now adds data stories, caching and tracked changes with rollback. Certified queries, which let a data team approve the metrics everyone should use, and live sharing remain in beta.

Claude Meetings takes notes, answers questions and builds docs, whiteboards and slides while the call is still going.

Who gets it

Whiteboards, Sheets and Data are available on all paid plans, on web, desktop and mobile. Claude Data needs a data connector, such as a warehouse or CRM. On Team plans all three are on by default. On Enterprise, Whiteboards and Sheets start switched off, and Data turns on by default on November 20, 2026. Claude Meetings is limited to Team and Enterprise plans, in Claude and Claude Desktop, and works only with Google Meet.

What this means if you pick tools for office work

Don't drop Excel yet. Sheets is a quick way to start a tracker or a budget inside a conversation, but Anthropic itself describes it as simple for now. If your work depends on advanced spreadsheet features, keep Excel, and look at Microsoft Copilot if you want AI inside Office.

Most people still need a separate note-taker. Claude Meetings is a beta on business plans only, and only for Google Meet. If you are on Pro or Max, or your calls run on Zoom or Microsoft Teams, a dedicated tool remains the practical choice: see our Otter.ai vs Fireflies.ai comparison and the guide to AI for meeting notes.

Teams already on Claude gain the most. If your company runs Claude Team or Enterprise, these features cut copy-pasting between Claude and other apps. Admins can switch them on or off in Organization settings under Artifacts. Treat the betas as previews and check the results before you share them.
