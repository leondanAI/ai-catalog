OpenAI Releases GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol: a Cheaper Frontier Model at $8/$40 and an Everyday Model at $2/$10

Models Nov 17, 2026·OpenAI Blog

OpenAI released two new models on November 17: GPT-6.2 Astra, its frontier model for the hardest projects, and GPT-6.2 Sol, an efficient model for everyday work. OpenAI's main pitch is efficiency: both models produce better results on demanding tasks while using fewer output tokens, so the same plan limit or API budget covers more work. The update comes three weeks after GPT-6.1 Terra, the cheaper model in the same family.

Where you can use it

In ChatGPT: both models are available to Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and in Codex; Enterprise and Edu users get them once a workspace admin turns them on. OpenAI's release notes describe them as a preview in Work and the Codex desktop app, and say regular Chat in ChatGPT keeps its existing model choices. Free and Go plans are not mentioned.
Ultrafast mode: Pro 500 subscribers can use it with either model, and for the first time Pro 200 subscribers get it too, starting with Sol.
In the API: the model IDs are gpt-6.2-astra and gpt-6.2-sol.

Prices

GPT-6.2 Astra costs $8 per million input tokens, $0.40 for cached input and $40 for output. That is 20% below GPT-6 Astra's $10/$50, and cached input is 60% cheaper. GPT-6.2 Sol keeps GPT-6.1 Sol's rates: $2 input, $0.10 cached input and $10 output per million tokens.

What OpenAI claims

These are OpenAI's own figures, not independent tests:

Professional work: on Agents' Last Exam, Astra scores 5.2 points above GPT-6 Astra at less than a third of the cost, and 5.5 points above Claude Fable 5.2 at about 5% of the cost. OpenAI says Astra's slides, spreadsheets and documents follow templates and industry conventions more closely; it can copy a slide template from a file or even a screenshot.
Sol for everyday tasks: on the FrontierCode 2.0 coding test, Sol comes within 0.5 points of Astra at about one-fifth of the cost. On LifeVal, a test of everyday tasks like planning meals or shortlisting homes, Sol beats GPT-6 Astra at about one-seventh of the cost.
3D and web: in a blind comparison of interactive 3D experiences, Astra ranks first (1,318 Elo), ahead of Claude Fable 5.2 (1,271) and Sol (1,259).

Tighter safety limits

Both models meet OpenAI's "Critical" threshold for cybersecurity, so they launch with stricter safeguards that, OpenAI admits, also block some legitimate security research. Astra is the company's first public model rated "High" for its ability to speed up AI research. In two tests, both models were more likely to stay silent about obstacles such as a missing file; OpenAI added instructions in Codex to reduce this.

What this means if you are choosing an AI tool

Start with Sol. For most everyday and coding work, OpenAI's own numbers put Sol close to Astra for a fraction of the price; keep Astra for long, complex projects. On a free plan, nothing changes yet: the new models are in Work and Codex on paid plans only. Comparing assistants?
