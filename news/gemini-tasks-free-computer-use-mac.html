Tasks in Gemini Go Free, Can Call Businesses and Pay With Google Pay; Gemini for Mac Gets Computer Use

Tools Nov 19, 2026·Gemini Apps release notes

Google spent the week of November 17 turning Gemini from a chatbot into an assistant that does things on its own. Two updates in the Gemini app release notes stand out: Tasks in Gemini became free, and the Mac app learned to operate other programs.

Tasks in Gemini, now free

Tasks in Gemini run in the background and handle multi-step projects and recurring routines, then notify you when results are ready. Google's examples: a daily morning briefing built from your email and calendar, a trip itinerary, repetitive research. Since November 17 the feature is rolling out free of charge to users in the US, India, Canada, Australia, Japan and Korea, running on the new Hydrogen 4 model (covered in our Gemini 4 Hydrogen story).

Four new ways to use Tasks arrived at the same time, all in English for US users aged 18+ with personal Google accounts:

Texting: after a one-time phone verification at gemini.google.com/channels, you can send requests from Google Messages or iMessage, for example a photo of a school flyer to add dates to Google Calendar, and steer a running task by replying.
Email: forward threads and PDFs to gemini@google.com from Gmail, Apple Mail or Outlook; Gemini replies to you privately with a link to review the results.
Phone calls: Gemini can call US businesses for you, several at once, to check stock, compare quotes or negotiate bills. You approve the call plan first and get a summary and full transcript. Gemini introduces itself as an AI assistant, won't share payment details by phone, and businesses can opt out.
Google Pay checkout: Gemini can watch for restocks and price drops and reorder household staples within a price cap you set. It prepares the checkout and sends an itemized summary; nothing is bought until you approve with one tap on your device, and payment uses a single-use virtual card.

Computer use in Gemini for Mac

On November 19 Google added computer use to the Gemini app for Mac. Gemini can click, type and fill in forms across desktop apps and websites in its own separate window, so you can keep using your screen and cursor, watch a live preview and stop it with one click. You choose which apps it may use; it pauses for logins and payments and may ask before sensitive actions. It is rolling out in English to Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra subscribers in the US.

Who it matters to

Free Tasks put background agents in front of far more people than paid plans did, and the call and checkout features go further than most rivals in acting in the real world, with an approval step before money moves. Computer use on Mac puts Gemini alongside Anthropic's and OpenAI's desktop agents; Claude opened its own computer use to Team and Enterprise plans the same week.
