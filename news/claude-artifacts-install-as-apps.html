Claude Can Now Turn Its Artifacts Into Desktop Apps for Mac and Windows

Tools Nov 24, 2026·Claude Help Center

Anthropic added three features to Claude on November 24. The most noticeable one: an artifact that Claude builds for you, such as a timer, a small dashboard or a file-renaming tool, can now be installed as an app on your Mac or Windows computer.

Artifacts as apps

An installed artifact gets its own window and icon and opens like any other program: on a Mac from the Claude Apps folder, Spotlight or the Dock, on Windows from the Start menu or a desktop shortcut. It stays the same artifact, so when you or another editor changes it in Claude, the app updates too. To open it, you need to be online and signed in to Claude Desktop.

To install one, open the artifact in Claude Desktop, open its title menu (or right-click it) and choose Install as app. Anthropic's examples of what to ask for include a Pomodoro timer in the menu bar, a Mac app that reminds you to stretch every hour, and a Windows app that renames photos in a folder by date.

The feature is in beta on Free, Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and Enterprise plans and needs Claude for Mac or Windows version 6.28899.0 or later. On Team plans it is on by default; on Enterprise plans an owner has to switch it on.

Permissions

An app can use Claude and your connectors the same way the artifact does. Anything beyond that requires a separate "Allow": notifications, global keyboard shortcuts, floating over other windows, a menu bar or system tray icon, and the top tier, full access to the computer, which lets the app run programs and read, change or delete files. Each "Allow" covers one version of the app, so after any edit Claude asks again.

Anthropic is explicit that it does not review apps before you install them and warns to grant full access only if you trust everyone who wrote the app. Claude shows a warning when an app comes from outside your organization or its author can't be confirmed, and on Team plans full access stays off until an admin enables it.

Two more updates

Monthly recap (Reflect, in Settings) is out of beta on Free, Pro, Max and Ultra plans on the web, desktop and mobile. Recaps can now include your Claude Code sessions and cloud Cowork tasks, and stored recaps can be deleted.

Files and connectors: Claude can now send a file to an app you have connected, or save a file that app offers. This is rolling out gradually in beta on Pro, Max and Ultra, and on Team and Enterprise when an owner enables it.

A few days earlier, on November 20, Anthropic also began rolling out a refreshed look for the Claude mobile app (starting with iOS 26 and later) and opened computer use in beta to Team and Enterprise plans.

Who it matters to

Installable artifacts move Claude closer to a tool for building small personal utilities without writing code yourself, a niche where people otherwise reach for app builders like Lovable or Replit. The difference is that a Claude app runs on your own computer and can be given access to it, which is why the permission prompts deserve a careful read.
