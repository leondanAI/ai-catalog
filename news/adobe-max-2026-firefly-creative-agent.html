Adobe MAX 2026: Firefly Gets an AI Agent and Models From OpenAI, Google and Kling

Tools Nov 10, 2026·Adobe Newsroom

Adobe used its MAX conference in Miami Beach on November 10 to reposition Adobe Firefly as one place to plan, generate and edit images, video and designs, with models from Adobe and other companies side by side. The centrepiece is Adobe Creative Agent, which now powers the Firefly AI Assistant, still in beta.

What changed in Firefly

The assistant gains three controls. Plan Mode shows the agent's plan so you can review, edit and approve it before anything runs, or let it work on its own. The Model Selector lets you pick a model for a session or switch to Auto-Select, where the agent chooses based on the output you want. Saved preferences keep formats, colours, brand guidelines, characters and products for reuse across projects. Always-on agents that handle recurring work in the background are in preview.

New partner models include OpenAI's GPT Image 2.6 (in Sunburst and Flare versions), Google's Nano Banana 2.1, Kling 4.0 and ByteDance's Dreamina Seedance 2.5. Topaz Labs adds Wonder 4 and Image HDR for upscaling, denoising and sharpening, and ElevenLabs is also among the partners. If you already generate images with ChatGPT Images or Nano Banana, OpenAI and Google models are now among the options inside Firefly.

Four tools are now generally available: Edit Text, Retouch, Relight and Quick Actions, a set of ready workflows such as Remove Object, Thumbnail Generator and Build Product Ad. Firefly Design, which puts illustration, image editing, animation, 3D and print on one canvas, is in beta, as is Firefly Graph for building repeatable workflows. Firefly Builder, which turns a text idea into a prototype website, presentation or app, is in private beta with a waitlist.

Beyond Firefly

AI Assistant (beta) expands to Premiere, Illustrator, After Effects, InDesign and Frame.io. Photoshop gets Relight and Generative Restyle, and Premiere gets a new colour grading system called Color Mode. Adobe is also previewing local MCP integrations for Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere and After Effects: an outside AI agent you already use can change your project files on your computer, while layers, sequences and compositions stay editable. Adobe did not say which agents are supported.

Availability and price

Adobe announced no new prices or credit changes and points to its Firefly pricing page for plan details. Features marked beta or preview may still change before general release.

What this means if you pay for several image and video generators

One subscription may now cover more. If you pay separately for an image generator and a video generator, Firefly could replace some of them, since you can switch between OpenAI, Google, Kling and ByteDance models in one place. Our ChatGPT Images vs Adobe Firefly comparison shows where each still fits better.

Check costs before you rely on partner models. Adobe gave no new figures, so look up on the Firefly pricing page what partner-model generations cost on your plan before planning heavy use.
