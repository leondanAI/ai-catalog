Midjourney V8.1 Brings Native 2K Rendering, Raw Mode, and Faster HD Generation

Models May 10, 2026·Midjourney Blog

Midjourney released V8.1 on April 30, 2026, after an alpha version shipped on April 14. The update delivers native 2K resolution output and a new Raw mode for literal prompt interpretation as the platform continues to refine its core image-generation model.

Native 2K and speed improvements

Native 2K resolution means V8.1 generates images at 2048px on the long edge without upscaling — the output is produced at full quality at generation time rather than being upscaled from a lower-resolution base. Midjourney says V8.1 HD generation is 3x faster and 3x cheaper than V8, while standard-resolution generation in V8.1 is 50% faster than V8 and runs about as fast as V7's draft mode. Midjourney cites roughly 4 seconds for standard-resolution generations and about 12 seconds for HD. This speed increase significantly improves iteration speed during the creative exploration phase.

Raw mode

Raw mode shifts V8.1 toward more literal prompt interpretation — reducing the aesthetic "opinionatedness" that Midjourney models are known for. In Raw mode, the model applies less automatic stylization and renders prompts more directly as described. This is particularly useful for technical illustrations, product mockups, and use cases where Midjourney's default artistic interpretation conflicts with the desired output.

What's next

V8.2 has been announced as the next planned release, continuing Midjourney's iterative rollout of updates to its core generation model.

Why it matters

V8.1's speed and resolution improvements make Midjourney more competitive with faster generators like Flux and Ideogram while retaining its distinctive aesthetic quality. The addition of a Raw mode also gives professional and technical users more control over how literally the model follows a prompt, broadening the range of use cases the platform can serve.
