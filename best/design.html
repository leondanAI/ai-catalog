Best AI design tools

Design AI ranges from all-in-one editors for social graphics to tools that turn a prompt into a UI layout, a website or a logo. Choose by the output you need and whether you must hand off editable files to a designer or developer.

Picked by AItoolFit · tool data checked 2026-10-30
1
AI design, agentic editing, brand consistency, web experiences

Canva AI

Free plan available👥 220M+

Canva AI 2.0 is the conversational core of Canva's Magic Studio, introduced at Canva Create 2026: you describe a design in text or voice and it returns an editable layout, built on Canva's own Design Model. Canva Craft, Canva's first in-house image-generation model, launched inside Dream Lab on October 22, 2026.

Open Canva AI → Canva AI review
2
UI/UX design, AI agents on canvas, image tools, voice input, text on path

Figma AI

Free plan available👥 5M+

Figma AI is the set of AI features built into Figma, governed by a shared AI-credit system that meters usage across Figma Make, First Draft, image generation and more. Every plan includes a monthly credit allotment, from 500 on the free Starter tier up to 4,250 on Enterprise Full.

Open Figma AI → Figma AI review
3
AI creative agent, Creative Cloud orchestration, image generation, sound generation

Adobe Firefly

Free plan available👥 10M+

Adobe Firefly is a generative AI creative studio for image, video, and audio, built on Adobe's own Firefly Image Model 5 (GA March 2026) alongside partner models from vendors such as Google, Runway, Kling, Luma, and ElevenLabs.

Open Adobe Firefly → Adobe Firefly review
4
E-commerce product photos: background removal, AI backgrounds and models, batch editing, API

Photoroom

Free plan available👥 80M+

Photoroom is an AI photo editor for product photography that removes backgrounds, adds shadows and AI-generated scenes, and batch-edits catalog images on mobile, web and through an API. The company says it is trusted by more than 80 million users and 1 million businesses.

Open Photoroom → Photoroom review
5
Free AI UI design and prototyping from text, voice or image prompts, with infinite canvas

Google Stitch

Free

Google Stitch is a Google Labs experiment that launched as a free tool for turning text, voice or image prompts into UI designs and front-end code. A May 2026 update added real-time canvas streaming, a conversational Stitch Agent, and new export options.

Open Google Stitch → Google Stitch review
6
AI-generated websites with designer-quality output and CMS

Framer AI

Free plan available👥 1M+

Framer is an AI-first website builder, now at version 3.0 (launched June 16, 2026), built around Framer Agents that co-edit your site directly on the canvas. AI work is metered through a credit system, and the tool runs from a free plan up to Enterprise.

Open Framer AI → Framer AI review
7
AI-generated visual designs, presentations, and prototypes via conversation

Claude Design

Free plan available👥 Free / from $20/mo

Claude Design is a built-in Claude feature, available on every Claude plan including Free, that generates UI mockups, presentations, dashboards-style visuals and prototypes directly inside a Claude conversation, Claude Code, or the Artifacts tab.

Open Claude Design → Claude Design review
8
AI-generated flowcharts, mind maps, and wireframes from text

Whimsical AI

Free plan available👥 4M+

Visual collaboration tool for flowcharts, mind maps, wireframes, and docs, with AI diagram generation from text prompts that draws on a metered credit allowance. Free plan available with usage caps; Pro starts at $10 per editor per month billed annually ($12 per editor per month billed monthly).

Open Whimsical AI → Whimsical AI review
9
AI logo and brand identity generation for small businesses

Looka

Free plan available👥 2M+

Looka is an AI logo maker and branding platform. You can generate, customize and preview logos for free, but downloading any file requires a one-time purchase or an annual subscription.

Open Looka → Looka review
10
AI UX design with wireframes, screen flows, and predictive heatmaps

UXPilot

Free plan available👥 1M+

UX Pilot is an AI design tool that generates and edits UI screens, runs UX research and attention prediction, and turns designs into HTML. It works in the browser and inside Figma through its Nodey plugin, using a credit-based freemium model.

Open UXPilot → UXPilot review
11
AI UI prototyping from screenshots, sketches, and text prompts

Uizard

Free plan available👥 300K+

AI UI design and prototyping tool that converts screenshots, sketches, and text prompts into editable app mockups. Acquired by Miro in 2024; still sold as a standalone product under the Uizard name.

Open Uizard → Uizard review
12
AI-generated website briefs, sitemaps, and wireframes with a component library for Webflow, Figma, and React, plus an in-beta feature to publish the live site directly

Relume

Free plan available👥 200K+

Relume is an AI tool that generates website briefs, sitemaps, and wireframes from a text description, backed by a component library for Webflow, Figma, and React. It also offers an in-beta Publish path that can host the finished site on a custom domain, in addition to exporting to Webflow or Figma for development.

Open Relume → Relume review
13
Instant AI background removal from photos and product images

Remove.bg

Free plan available👥 150M+

Remove.bg is a Canva-owned AI tool that automatically removes backgrounds from images, but the standalone website, accounts, API, and apps are shutting down on December 1, 2026, with the technology moving into Canva and its API moving to Leonardo.ai.

Open Remove.bg → Remove.bg review
