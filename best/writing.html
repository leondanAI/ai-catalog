Best AI writing tools

Writing tools either improve text you have already written — grammar, style, clarity, translation — or generate new copy from a brief. For most people an editing tool plus a general AI assistant covers more than a dedicated copy generator.

Picked by AItoolFit · tool data checked 2026-10-06
1
Professional-grade AI translation for documents and business content

DeepL

Free plan available👥 100M+

DeepL is a translation service built on its own specialized translation LLM, covering 100+ languages for text and offering a separate real-time voice-to-voice mode. It has a free plan plus paid Individual, Team, Business, and Enterprise tiers billed per user.

Open DeepL → DeepL review
2
AI writing assistant for grammar, style, tone, and clarity improvements

Grammarly

Free plan available👥 40M+

Grammarly is an AI writing assistant for grammar, clarity, tone and rewriting that works across the web and desktop apps. Since 2025 it is part of the Superhuman suite, with prompt-based AI generation and the proactive Superhuman Go agent alongside the classic checker.

Open Grammarly → Grammarly review
3
AI paraphrasing, summarizing, and grammar checking for students and writers

QuillBot

Paid only👥 50M+

QuillBot is an AI writing platform built around paraphrasing, summarizing, and grammar checking, with added translation, citation, and plagiarism/AI-content detection tools for students and writers.

Open QuillBot → QuillBot review
4
Plain language writing — identifies complex sentences and passive voice

Hemingway Editor

Free plan available

A readability editor that flags hard-to-read sentences, passive voice and adverbs. The current flagship, Hemingway Editor Plus, adds an AI layer with sentence rewrites, grammar fixes and tone adjustment on top of the long-standing free online highlighter.

Open Hemingway Editor → Hemingway Editor review
5
AI marketing copy, brand voice, campaign content, no-code agents

Jasper

Paid only👥 70K+

Jasper is an AI marketing platform for content, campaigns and brand-consistent copy, now positioned as an agent workspace offering 100+ specialized marketing AI agents alongside a GEO (generative engine optimization) agent.

Open Jasper → Jasper review
6
AI content writing, SEO articles, competitor analysis, search visibility

Writesonic

Paid only👥 200K+

Writesonic is now an AI Search Visibility platform. Its Article Writer produces long, research-backed articles with competitor analysis alongside tools for tracking brand presence in AI search results.

Open Writesonic → Writesonic review
