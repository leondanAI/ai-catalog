Best AI productivity tools

Productivity AI takes over routine work around meetings, notes and scheduling: it records and summarizes calls, plans your calendar and connects the apps you already use. The best choice depends on where your team works — video calls, docs or task lists.

Notes, knowledge base, docs, developer platform, AI agents

Notion AI

Paid only👥 100M+

Notion AI is the assistant built into the Notion workspace. Its October 2026 updates turned it into an agent system that connects to outside AI assistants via MCP, syncs Skills through a Skills API, draws on models from several AI vendors, and now includes Notion Agents with their own computer and browser for tasks lasting up to 24 hours.

AI meeting transcription, video replay, sales intelligence, CRM sync

Otter.ai

Free plan available👥 10M+

Otter.ai is a voice-productivity platform that, in an April 2026 press release, repositioned itself from a passive meeting notetaker into an agentic Conversational Knowledge Engine, adding voice-activated AI agents for meetings, sales, and demos alongside its transcription roots. Its current release centers on Otter Meeting Agents plus a rebuilt, private-by-default AI Chat.

Open Otter.ai → Otter.ai review
3
Bot-free AI meeting notes, pre-meeting briefs, chat across meetings, MCP for AI tools

Granola

Free plan available👥 Free / from $14/mo

Granola is an AI notepad that transcribes meetings from your device's audio without a bot joining the call, then combines your rough notes and the transcript into structured notes, action items and follow-ups. It runs on Mac, Windows, iPhone, Android and Apple Watch.

Open Granola → Granola review
4
AI-powered workflow automation connecting 9,000+ apps

Zapier AI

Free plan available👥 2M+

Zapier is a workflow automation platform connecting 9,000+ apps, with Zapier Copilot building automations from plain language, AI by Zapier running AI-driven steps inside Zaps, and Zapier MCP exposing Zapier's actions to external LLMs such as Claude and ChatGPT.

Open Zapier AI → Zapier AI review
5
AI meeting notes, transcription, action item extraction, and system-wide voice dictation

Fireflies.ai

Free plan available👥 20M+

Fireflies.ai is an AI meeting assistant that records, transcribes and summarizes calls across Zoom, Google Meet, Teams and more. Since September 2026 it also offers Fireflies Talk, free unlimited voice dictation into any desktop app on Mac and Windows, available on every plan including Free.

Open Fireflies.ai → Fireflies.ai review
6
AI calendar optimization — automatically schedules focus time and habits

Reclaim.ai

Free plan available👥 100K+

Reclaim.ai is an AI scheduling assistant that automatically fits tasks, habits, and meetings into your calendar. Paid plans are built around per-seat AI Agents entitlements across Starter, Business, and Enterprise tiers.

Open Reclaim.ai → Reclaim.ai review
7
AI calendar and task scheduler that builds your daily plan automatically

Motion

Paid only

Motion is an AI-powered work platform that combines automatic task scheduling and calendar management with AI chat, docs, meeting notes, dashboards, and AI agents. It now positions itself as an "AI Powered SuperApp for Work" rather than a standalone project manager.
