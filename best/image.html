Best AI image generators

Image generators differ in photorealism, how well they render text, how precisely they follow a prompt and how much control you get when editing. Some run in a chat window, others give you a full canvas or run locally on your own computer.

Image generation and precise editing inside ChatGPT, text and UI rendering

ChatGPT Images

Free plan available👥 Free / from $20/mo

OpenAI's image generation in ChatGPT and the API. The current line is GPT Image 2.5, released September 8, 2026, in two variants: Flare, the fastest for everyday generation, and Sunburst, the most capable for precise editing. Both cost $8 per million image input tokens and $30 per million output.

Nano Banana

Free plan available

Nano Banana is Google's family of Gemini image generation and editing models, including Nano Banana 2, Nano Banana 2 Lite, Nano Banana 2.1 and Nano Banana Pro, available through the Gemini app, Google AI Studio and the Gemini API.

Paid only👥 1M+

Paid plans add credits, unlimited generation on selected models and a commercial license.

open-source AI image generation, node workflows, local GPU, 4K video

ComfyUI

Free plan available👥 Free / from $20/mo

ComfyUI is an open-source, node-based visual editor for building image, video and other generative AI pipelines using diffusion models and partner API nodes, with new releases issued roughly weekly. It is available as a free self-hosted app or as the managed Comfy Cloud service for those who want GPU compute without local setup.

AI image & video generation, native audio, text rendering, multi-reference editing, self-hostable open-weight models

FLUX

Free plan available

Flux is an image, video, and world-model family from Black Forest Labs. The current generation is FLUX 3 (image, video with native audio, an open-weights Action/robotics model, and the open-weights Image Klein variant), with the FLUX.2 lineup still offered alongside it.

Stable Diffusion

Free plan available

Stable Diffusion is Stability AI's open-weights text-to-image model. The current line is Stable Diffusion 3.5 (Large 8.1B, Large Turbo, and Medium 2.5B), free to self-host under the Community License for anyone under $1M in annual revenue, with a paid hosted API for teams that prefer not to run it themselves.

Recraft AI is a design-focused image generator built around its Recraft V4.1 model family, including a fast Flash variant. It produces photorealistic raster images, true SVG vectors, icons, mockups and product shots from text prompts.
