Best AI data analysis tools

Data AI tools let you ask questions in plain language, build charts and dashboards, or analyze spreadsheets without writing code. Choose by where your data lives and whether you need shared dashboards for a team or a one-off analysis.

Picked by AItoolFit · tool data checked 2026-10-25
1
Business dashboards and reports with Copilot for questions, summaries and DAX

Microsoft Power BI

Free plan available

Microsoft Power BI is a business intelligence tool for building interactive reports and dashboards, with Copilot to answer data questions, summarize reports and write DAX. Sharing requires Power BI Pro, billed annually at $14 per user per month, and Copilot requires a paid Fabric (F2 or higher) or Premium (P1 or higher) capacity.

Open Microsoft Power BI → Microsoft Power BI review
2
Free-to-start, Google-integrated BI dashboards and reports (the product formerly called Looker Studio, renamed back to Data Studio in April 2026)

Data Studio (Looker Studio)

Free plan available

Data Studio (formerly Looker Studio, originally Google Data Studio) is Google's business intelligence tool for building interactive dashboards and reports from more than 1,400 data connectors, with a free self-service tier and an optional paid Pro tier. A natural-language Conversational Analytics preview is now available to all users, while some Gemini-assisted features remain tied to the paid tier.

Open Data Studio (Looker Studio) → Data Studio (Looker Studio) review
3
Enterprise BI with AI-powered analytics — Salesforce-integrated

Tableau AI

Paid only

Tableau Agent is Salesforce's generative-AI assistant for Tableau (formerly Einstein Copilot for Tableau), adding conversational, natural-language analytics and agentic authoring across Tableau's editions and Tableau Next.

Open Tableau AI → Tableau AI review
4
Building internal AI tools and apps on top of databases and APIs

Retool AI

Free plan available

Platform for building internal business tools — dashboards, admin panels, data editors — now built around an AI-native app builder that generates a working app from a description, plus a classic drag-and-drop builder and agentic workflows. Free plan covers up to 5 users; paid tiers separate Builder and Internal User seats, billed monthly or annually (Team Builder seats $10/mo annual or $12/mo monthly; Business Builder seats $50/mo annual or $65/mo monthly).

Open Retool AI → Retool AI review
5
Collaborative AI data notebooks and apps for analytics teams

Hex

Free plan available

Collaborative data notebook and app-building platform with a built-in AI agent for SQL and Python. Community tier is free with limited features; Professional and Team plans are billed monthly per Editor seat, with GPU compute available as a separate pay-as-you-go add-on.

Open Hex → Hex review
6
Conversational AI data analysis — upload any dataset, ask questions in plain language

Julius AI

Free plan available

Julius AI is an AI-powered data agent platform that connects to CSV files and live databases (Postgres, BigQuery, Snowflake) and uses third-party language models from providers including OpenAI and Anthropic to analyze data, build charts, and produce slides and reports. It runs on a credit-based pricing model with a free plan and several paid tiers.

Open Julius AI → Julius AI review
7
AI workflow automation for media agencies and data providers (strategy, audience segmentation, media planning, activation, measurement)

Akkio

Paid only

Akkio is an AI workflow automation platform built for media agencies and data providers, covering strategy, audience segmentation, media planning, activation and measurement. Pricing is enterprise-only and custom, with no public rates or free trial.

Open Akkio → Akkio review
