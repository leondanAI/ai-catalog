Best AI voice and audio tools

Audio AI covers voice generation and cloning, speech-to-text, music creation and cleanup of recorded speech. Most tools specialise in one of these, so pick the task first, then compare voice quality, languages and the license for commercial use.

Picked by AItoolFit · tool data checked 2026-10-25
1
Voice cloning, TTS, music and sound effects, image and video generation, lip sync, real-time voice agents with a choice of LLM

ElevenLabs

Free plan available

ElevenLabs is a multimodal AI studio on a single credit balance: speech, voice cloning, music, sound effects, transcription, dubbing, and image and video generation with lip sync. It runs its own Eleven v4 and Eleven v4 Turbo voice models and aggregates third-party models from ByteDance, Google, OpenAI, Black Forest Labs and others, so one subscription covers most media formats.

Open ElevenLabs → ElevenLabs review
2
AI music generation, voice cloning, custom model fine-tuning, and spoken-voice generation with Suno Speech (beta)

Suno

Free plan available👥 12M+

Suno is an AI tool that turns a text prompt into a full song with vocals and instruments, and now also offers Suno Speech (beta), which generates spoken voice paired with original background music as one track. The current model is v6 (with a v6-wild variant) on paid plans, while free accounts run the lighter v6-mini model.

Open Suno → Suno review
3
AI video editing, voice cloning, dubbing, MCP automation

Descript

Free plan available👥 6M+

Descript is a transcript-based audio and video editor whose current centerpiece is Underlord (2026), a generative AI co-editor that drafts scripts, writes show notes, finds shareable clips, and generates and extends video.

Open Descript → Descript review
4
Open-source speech-to-text transcription — developer tool, not a consumer product

Whisper

Free plan available

Whisper is OpenAI's open-source speech-to-text model for transcription and translation. The latest open checkpoint is large-v3-turbo, an optimized version of large-v3 that runs roughly 8x faster with only a small accuracy trade-off.

Open Whisper → Whisper review
5
Text-to-speech reading assistant for documents, PDFs, and web pages

Speechify

Free plan available👥 20M+

Speechify is a text-to-speech and voice-AI platform built on its proprietary SIMBA 3.x voice models. It reads text aloud in 1000+ voices at up to 5x speed and also offers a Voice AI Assistant, AI Podcasts, Voice Typing, and a developer Voice API.

Open Speechify → Speechify review
6
Professional AI voiceovers for e-learning, explainer videos, and presentations

Murf AI

Free plan available👥 10M+

AI voice platform built around a studio-style voiceover editor (200+ voices, 30+ languages and accents) for e-learning, explainer videos, and presentations, alongside a separate conversational voice-agent product and a low-latency text-to-speech API (Falcon).

Open Murf AI → Murf AI review
7
AI audio enhancement and recording for podcasters and content creators

Adobe Podcast

Free plan available

Adobe Podcast is a browser-based audio tool whose flagship feature, Enhance Speech, cleans up spoken recordings to sound studio-quality. A free tier is available, with fuller access sold through Adobe Firefly plans starting at $9.99/month.

Open Adobe Podcast → Adobe Podcast review
8
Streaming-only AI music generation with high sonic quality — no downloads currently available on any tier

Udio

Free plan available

AI music generator known for high-fidelity audio quality. Following a partnership with Universal Music Group, downloading of generated audio, video, and stems is currently disabled platform-wide, including on paid plans.
