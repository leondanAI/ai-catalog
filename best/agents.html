Best AI agents and automation tools

This category covers two kinds of tools: workflow builders that connect your apps and run steps automatically, and agents that take a goal and work out the steps themselves. Builders are more predictable for repeat tasks; agents save time on one-off research and multi-step jobs.

Picked by AItoolFit · tool data checked 2026-11-10
1
AI workflow automation, self-hosted, node editor, human-in-the-loop

n8n

Free plan available

n8n is a fair-code workflow automation platform for connecting apps, APIs and AI agents, self-hosted or via managed cloud. As of early November 2026 the stable line is n8n 2.46, with n8n 3.0 and 3.1 still in pre-release and the older 1.x line still receiving separate patches.

2
Enterprise agents, Microsoft ecosystem

Microsoft Copilot Studio

Paid only👥 from $200/mo

Microsoft Copilot Studio is a low-code platform for building and deploying custom AI agents that connect to Microsoft 365, Dynamics 365 and external systems. It lets makers choose among models from vendors including OpenAI and Anthropic, and is expanding to build apps from natural language and give agents their own Microsoft 365 identities.

Open Microsoft Copilot Studio → Microsoft Copilot Studio review
3
Autonomous AI agent, mobile/web app building, task automation

Manus

Paid only👥 from $20/mo

Manus is an independently operated autonomous AI agent platform, now on version 2.0, which introduces the Cascade agent architecture, Cloud Computer, Automations, and Manus Studio for building web, mobile, and creative projects with minimal human supervision.

Open Manus → Manus review
4
Autonomous AI software engineer, junior dev task automation

Devin

Free plan available👥 Free / from $20/mo

Devin is Cognition's autonomous AI software engineer that plans, writes, tests and reviews code largely on its own. The current release is Devin 2.2, running on the Devin Fusion multi-model architecture with the Devin Security Swarm for vulnerability fixing.

Open Devin → Devin review
5
Complex automations, app integrations

Make

Free plan available👥 Free / from $9/mo

Make is a visual, no-code automation platform for building workflows across 3,000+ apps, with Make AI Agents, a feature currently in beta for building autonomous AI agents inside scenarios.

Open Make → Make review
6
Email agents, scheduling, support bots

Lindy

Paid only👥 from $29.99/mo

Lindy is an AI agent-building platform now positioned as an 'AI teammate,' with a per-user Team plan starting at $29.99/month for 3,000 credits and a custom Enterprise tier. There is no public version number, and it offers a 7-day free trial with $50 in credits rather than an ongoing free plan.

Open Lindy → Lindy review
7
Multi-agent teams, sales and support bots

Relevance AI

Paid only

No-code platform to build AI agents and multi-agent teams for business workflows.

Open Relevance AI → Relevance AI review
8
Autonomous tasks, open-source AI agents

AutoGPT

Free plan available👥 Free / from $50/mo

AutoGPT is an open-source platform for building and running autonomous AI agents. Now shipping as the AutoGPT Platform (beta v0.8.2, September 30, 2026), it pairs a visual Agent Builder and AI Experts with a managed cloud and paid Pro/Max subscriptions.
