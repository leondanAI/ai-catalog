Best AI video generators

AI video tools fall into three groups: models that generate footage from a prompt or image, avatar tools that turn a script into a presenter video, and editors that cut, caption and repurpose what you already have. Decide which of these jobs you need before comparing quality.

AI video editing, story generation, 2K/4K export

CapCut

Free plan available👥 800M+

CapCut is a cross-platform video editor for mobile, desktop and web. The 2026 release leans heavily on AI, adding ByteDance's Seedream image model and Seedance video generation tools alongside the traditional editor, with the most advanced tools and 4K export reserved for paid plans.

Short-form AI video clips, multi-shot consistency, enterprise video production

Kling AI

Paid only

Kling AI is a text- and image-to-video generator from Kuaishou. Kling 4.0 officially launched on October 15, 2026, with Kling 4.0 Flash in early access since September 28, 2026 for Ultra yearly subscribers; the legacy Kling 1.0 through 2.1 Master models, the Virtual Try-On API and the 119 effect templates were retired in September 2026.

Open Kling AI → Kling AI review
3
AI video and image ads, social clips and AI influencers using Kling, Seedance, Veo and Nano Banana in one place

Higgsfield

Free plan available👥 40M+

Higgsfield is an AI creative platform that bundles third-party video and image models, such as Kling, Seedance, Google Veo and Nano Banana, with its own tools for ads, social clips and AI influencers. The company says it has more than 40 million users and passed a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate in September 2026.

Open Higgsfield → Higgsfield review
4
AI video generation, native audio, image-to-video, 4K video output

Google Veo

Paid only

Google's text- and image-to-video model, currently shipping as Veo 3.1, generates short clips with synchronized dialogue, sound effects and ambient audio. It is available through Google Flow, Vertex AI and the Gemini API, with paid consumer plans and per-second API pricing.

Open Google Veo → Google Veo review
5
AI avatars, AI voice cloning, video dubbing, credit-based plans

HeyGen

Free plan available👥 2M+

HeyGen is an AI video platform for creating avatar-driven videos, voice cloning, and translation. Its flagship avatar model, Avatar V, builds a lifelike digital twin from a single 15-second recording.

Open HeyGen → HeyGen review
6
AI video with avatars, training videos, corporate communications, and quick marketing clips via Syren (beta)

Synthesia

Free plan available👥 50K+

Synthesia is an AI video platform that turns text scripts into narrated avatar videos. Its current release, Synthesia 3.0, uses the Express-2 full-body avatar engine and interactive Video Agents, and the platform has now added Syren, a beta tool that generates motion-graphics videos from a prompt, preset or branded document.

Open Synthesia → Synthesia review
7
AI video generation, multi-modal input, long single-pass clips, reference-based video editing

Seedance

Paid only

Seedance is ByteDance's AI video generation model line, now at version 2.5, which accepts text, image, audio and video inputs through ByteDance's own apps and the BytePlus ModelArk API as well as third-party platforms.

Open Seedance → Seedance review
8
AI video generation, temporal consistency, API access, creative workflows

Runway

Free plan available

Runway is a multi-model generative platform for image, video, and audio. Its current flagship is Gen-4.5, announced December 1, 2025, which ranked No. 1 on the Artificial Analysis Video Arena leaderboard and is available on all paid plans.

Open Runway → Runway review
9
AI video generation, native 1080p, character consistency, real-time rendering

Luma AI

Free plan available

Luma AI is a generative video platform built around its own Ray 3.2 and UNI-1.1 models. It now positions itself as a creative agent that unifies Luma's own and third-party image and video models into one workflow under a single credit pool, with connectors to Frame.io, Figma and Google Ads Console.

Open Luma AI → Luma AI review
10
Multi-model AI video, image, and audio generation, camera-direction control, developer API access

Pika

Paid only👥 1M+

Pika is a web-based AI creation platform for generating short video clips, images, and audio from text or image prompts, combining its own Pika 2.5 video model with third-party models from vendors including Google, ElevenLabs, and others.
