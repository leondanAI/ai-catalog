Best AI tools for learning

Learning AI adapts practice to your level, explains mistakes step by step and turns notes into flashcards or quizzes. Choose by subject and format: daily language practice, homework help or structured courses.

Picked by AItoolFit · tool data checked 2026-10-25
1
AI-powered language learning with conversation practice and explanations

Duolingo Max

Free plan available👥 100M+

Duolingo's premium AI tier: conversation practice, grammar explanations and roleplay scenarios powered by OpenAI's models. Explain My Answer is now free for all Duolingo users, while Video Call remains part of the paid Max tier in supported languages.

Open Duolingo Max → Duolingo Max review
2
Flashcards, AI study guides from notes and lectures, practice tests, spaced repetition

Quizlet

Free plan available

Quizlet is a study platform that turns notes, recorded lectures and your own material into flashcards, study guides, practice tests and games. Studying with flashcards is free, while Quizlet Plus adds more practice tests, Learn rounds, expert textbook solutions and ad-free studying.

Open Quizlet → Quizlet review
3
AI learning coach for Coursera courses with personalized guidance, career support, and interview practice

Coursera Coach

Paid only👥 148M+

Coursera Coach is an AI assistant built into the Coursera platform that offers in-course tutoring, career guidance, Socratic practice, and Role Play simulations for learners, plus authoring support for educators.

Open Coursera Coach → Coursera Coach review
4
Step-by-step math problem solving from camera or typed input

Photomath

Free plan available👥 100M+

Photomath is a Google-owned math app that scans handwritten or printed problems with your phone camera and shows step-by-step solutions. It has no formally published version number, and support is now hosted on Google's support site.

Open Photomath → Photomath review
5
AI-powered study assistant that learns from your own documents and videos

YouLearn

Free plan available👥 2M+

AI study tool that turns your own materials — PDFs, videos, YouTube links, web pages — into an interactive tutor. Ask questions, get summaries, and generate quizzes from your own content, with paid Pro, Max, and Teams plans plus a lower-cost usage tier.

Open YouLearn → YouLearn review
6
AI tutor from Khan Academy — Socratic method teaching for students

Khanmigo

Free plan available

Khan Academy's AI tutor, built on OpenAI's models via Microsoft's Azure OpenAI Service with Google Gemini powering some newer features. Uses the Socratic method — guiding students to answers through questions rather than giving them away. Free for teachers, with paid access for individual students and families.

Open Khanmigo → Khanmigo review
7
Adaptive math learning for kids ages 5–11 (K-5)

Synthesis

Paid only

Adaptive math learning platform (Synthesis Tutor) originally built for SpaceX employees' children, now open to the public. Covers K-5 math ages 5-11 through game-based challenges; a separate product, Synthesis Teams, covers ages 8-14.

Open Synthesis → Synthesis review
