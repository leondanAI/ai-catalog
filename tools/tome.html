Beautiful.ai is a web-based presentation tool whose slides auto-format as you add content, with a March 2026 update introducing a context-aware AI workflow for conversational deck creation. It is a paid product with a 14-day free trial and no permanent free plan.

Prezi Freemium
Live-session audience engagement and post-session learning content, with AI deck creation as an alternate starting point

Prezi is a presentation platform now built around audience engagement during a live session (polls, quizzes, Q&A, word clouds via one QR code) and post-session learning content (flashcards, self-tests, podcast, narrated video), with AI deck generation offered as one of two ways to start a presentation. Swoop, a separate AI agent built by Prezi, turns inputs from tools like Drive, Slack, Notion and Zoom into presentations and documents.
