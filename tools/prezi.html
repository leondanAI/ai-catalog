About Prezi

Prezi started as the app known for its zoomable, non-linear canvas, but the product most people use today is Prezi AI. Instead of just helping with layout, it builds an entire designed presentation from a one-line prompt or an uploaded document, usually in under about eight seconds and without forcing you through a separate outline step.

There are no formal version numbers for the consumer product anymore; Prezi ships continuously. The big shift is the move from a simple AI design assistant to full generative creation. You can paste a website URL and Prezi will pull the brand's colors, fonts and logo (it recognizes over 2,000 brand logos), then refine the deck through a chat interface with a rewind button to undo changes.

On capabilities, current Prezi AI generates funnels, roadmaps, processes and quote layouts, embeds YouTube video directly in slides, and now exports finished decks to PowerPoint (.PPTX) and PDF. The headline 2026 launch is Swoop, released on March 31, 2026: a standalone AI communication agent at swoop.it that aggregates many sources, Google Drive, Notion, Slack, Gmail, Zoom, Salesforce, HubSpot and meeting recordings, into presentations, documents and messages. Swoop also appears in the Grammarly and Superhuman Go Agent Store.

Pricing is freemium and billed annually. Basic is free but limits you to public presentations. Standard runs about $7 per user per month, Plus about $15, Premium about $25, and Teams about $39 per user per month. Education plans start around $3-4 per month, with EDU Teams near $19 per user per month, and paid plans include a 14-day free trial.

The main limitations are practical. The free tier keeps your work public, which rules it out for anything confidential. Exact prices also vary noticeably across third-party listings, so confirm the current figures on prezi.com before committing, and the zooming style, while distinctive, is not what every audience or corporate template expects.

Prezi suits people who want a designed deck fast without starting from a blank slide, teams that value on-brand output pulled straight from a URL, and anyone curious about Swoop's cross-app agent approach to turning scattered inputs into finished communication.

Advantages
  • Prezi AI builds a complete, designed deck from one prompt or a document in roughly eight seconds, skipping the manual outline step
  • Brand extraction from a pasted URL pulls colors, fonts and logos automatically, with recognition of 2,000+ brand logos
  • Chat-based refinement with a rewind button makes iterating on a deck fast and forgiving
  • Exports to PowerPoint (.PPTX) and PDF, plus embedded YouTube video, fit it into existing workflows
  • Swoop adds a genuinely new capability, pulling from Drive, Slack, Gmail, Zoom and more into finished output
Disadvantages
  • The free Basic tier only allows public presentations, so it is unsuitable for confidential work
  • Published pricing varies across third-party aggregators, making the real cost hard to pin down without checking the site
  • No formal version numbers make it hard to track exactly what changed and when
  • The signature zooming canvas can feel gimmicky and clashes with conventional corporate templates
  • Swoop is a separate product, so the full 2026 experience is split across two tools rather than one

Choose Prezi if…

  • You want a finished, designed deck generated from a single prompt or an existing document
  • You need on-brand slides pulled automatically from a company website URL
  • You already live in PowerPoint or PDF and need clean export from an AI-built deck
  • You want to try Swoop to turn Drive, Slack, Gmail and meeting recordings into presentations
  • You are fine keeping presentations public on the free plan or paying for privacy

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Prezi still relevant in 2026?
Prezi is still used and has added AI features. However, its zooming presentation style has become less fashionable, and newer tools like Gamma have surpassed it for AI generation quality and modern design. Prezi's main remaining differentiator is its unique non-linear presentation format.
Does Prezi have AI?
Yes. Prezi AI (launched 2023) can generate presentations from prompts and transform documents into presentations. The AI quality has improved but is generally considered below Gamma or Tome for design output.
Is Gamma free?
Gamma has a free plan with 400 AI credits — enough for several full presentations. Plus is $10/month and Pro is $20/month for unlimited AI generation, analytics, and custom domains.
What happened to Prezi?
Prezi remains active and profitable but has declined from its peak popularity around 2012-2016. It pivoted to Prezi Video (presenting with overlays on video calls) and Prezi Design, and added AI features. It still has millions of users, particularly in education.
