About Leonardo AI

Leonardo AI is an AI image and video generation platform used by game developers, concept artists, marketers, and creative studios. It offers a web-based interface alongside a robust API, combining proprietary model pipelines with third-party model integrations. The platform is built for high-volume creative production and professional asset generation workflows.

The Creative Engine API, launched April 14, 2026, is an enterprise-grade API that enables code extraction and replication of Leonardo's generation pipelines. Enterprises can use it to embed Leonardo's image and video generation capabilities into proprietary tools, internal platforms, and production workflows without exposing end users to the Leonardo interface directly.

Leonardo underwent a full rebrand in April 2026, adopting the identity "Yours to Create" developed by agency Koto. The rebrand accompanied the release of the new Alchemy v4 pipeline and the Phoenix architecture — updated generation models that improve quality on photorealistic and stylized outputs and reduce artifact rates on complex compositions.

Third-party model integrations expanded significantly: Veo 3, Sora 2, Kling, and Seedance are now accessible directly through Leonardo's interface and API, allowing users to run multiple leading video generation models from a single platform without separate subscriptions.

Motion 2.0, the platform's image-to-video system, received updates improving motion coherence across longer clips, enabling longer output durations, and adding improved lip sync for talking-head and character animation use cases. Sketch-to-image reached sub-second real-time update speeds, making it the fastest sketch-to-image implementation available as of May 2026.

Leonardo AI is best for creative professionals, game developers, and enterprise teams who need high-volume image and video generation with access to multiple leading models from a single API and interface.

Advantages
  • Creative Engine API: enterprise code extraction and replication of generation pipelines
  • Alchemy v4 and Phoenix architecture reduce artifacts on complex photorealistic outputs
  • Integrates Veo 3, Sora 2, Kling, and Seedance — multiple top models in one platform
  • Sketch-to-image with sub-second real-time updates — best-in-class speed as of May 2026
  • Motion 2.0: better motion coherence, longer clips, and improved lip sync for video
Disadvantages
  • Enterprise Creative Engine API pricing not publicly listed — requires sales contact
  • Multiple model integrations make the platform complex to navigate for new users
  • Credit consumption varies by model — Veo 3 and Sora 2 are significantly more expensive per generation
  • Free tier has limited daily token allocation — insufficient for professional volume use

Choose Leonardo AI if…

  • ✅ You create game assets, characters, or concept art needing consistency across many images
  • ✅ You want to fine-tune models on your own images for consistent style and character
  • ✅ You need built-in tools: canvas editor, background removal, and image-to-image generation
  • ✅ You want a more generous free tier — Leonardo offers 150 free tokens per day

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Leonardo.ai used for?
Leonardo.ai is an AI image generation platform popular with game developers, concept artists, and designers. It offers fine-tuning on custom image sets, a canvas editor, motion generation, and character reference features for maintaining consistency across a series of images.
Is Leonardo AI free?
Leonardo.ai has a free plan with 150 tokens per day (roughly 30 standard images). Apprentice Membership is $12/month, Artisan is $30/month, Maestro is $60/month, offering more tokens, priority generation, and additional features.
Is Leonardo.ai better than Stable Diffusion?
Leonardo.ai is built on top of Stable Diffusion but adds a curated platform experience, fine-tuned models, better UI, and community features. For users who don't want to set up local Stable Diffusion, Leonardo offers similar capabilities with significantly less technical setup.
Which is better for game assets — Leonardo or Midjourney?
Leonardo.ai is generally preferred for game asset production. Its fine-tuning capabilities ensure character and style consistency across multiple images. Midjourney produces higher quality individual images but lacks fine-tuning for maintaining consistent visual identity across a project.
