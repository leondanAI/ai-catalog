About Akkio

Akkio is an AI analytics platform aimed at media and advertising agencies. Rather than a general dashboard tool, it packages a suite of AI agents that handle the repetitive analytical work behind campaign planning, audience segmentation and reporting.

The product has changed direction. Akkio started life as a no-code AI/ML tool for business analysts building forecasting models from CSV or CRM data. As of mid-2026 it has repositioned squarely around agency workflows, and forecasting is now just one capability among many. There are no formal version numbers; the notable recent addition is the Media Planning Agent, which lets you upload reach curves, set goals and generate a baseline media mix you can edit inline.

The agent suite covers the main stages of agency work: an Audience Agent for segmentation, a Strategy Agent, Chat Explore and Chat Data Prep for conversational analysis and cleanup, Forecasting, and Generative Reports that write up findings automatically.

On pricing, the official site now shows only an Enterprise AI Analytics Platform with custom, contact-sales pricing and no public figures. Third-party directories still list older self-serve tiers: a view-only Free plan, Basic at $49/user/month, Pro at $99/user/month, and a Build-On package from $999/month with white-label and API access. A free trial is referenced, but since Akkio no longer publishes these amounts their current accuracy is uncertain.

The main limitations are the narrowed focus and the pricing opacity. If you are not an agency, the media-centric agents may be more than you need, and the shift to contact-sales makes it hard to gauge cost without a sales conversation.

Akkio suits media and advertising agencies that want to automate audience, planning and reporting work with AI agents, and enterprises comfortable with custom contracts. Analysts wanting a simple self-serve forecasting tool may find the product has moved past that use case.

Choose Akkio if…

  • You need a Media Planning Agent that builds an editable baseline media mix
  • You want conversational, generative reporting over your campaign data
  • You are comfortable with enterprise, contact-sales contracts

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Akkio?
Akkio is a no-code AI analytics platform designed for marketing agencies and business teams. It can build predictive models — churn prediction, lead scoring, forecasting — from CSV or connected data sources without requiring data science knowledge. It's designed to deliver AI insights to clients.
What is the difference between Akkio and Julius AI?
Akkio focuses on building reusable predictive models and deploying them for ongoing business use. Julius AI focuses on one-off conversational data analysis — ask questions, get answers, generate charts. Akkio is for ongoing ML models; Julius is for interactive data exploration.
Is Akkio free?
Akkio has a free trial. Paid plans start at around $49/month. It's priced for agencies and businesses that want to offer AI analytics to multiple clients.
Is Julius AI better than ChatGPT for data analysis?
Julius AI is purpose-built for data analysis with optimized data upload, persistent file context, and data-specific chart generation. ChatGPT's code interpreter does similar things but is more general-purpose. Julius has a cleaner data-focused UX for regular data analysis tasks.
