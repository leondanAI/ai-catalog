About Adobe Podcast

Adobe Podcast (now part of Adobe Express) is a free AI audio enhancement tool that makes any voice recording sound like it was captured in a professional recording studio — regardless of the actual microphone or recording environment used. The core feature, Enhance Speech, removes background noise, echo, room reverb, and audio artifacts from any uploaded recording in under 30 seconds, requiring no technical audio knowledge.

The results are genuinely remarkable: a phone recording made in a noisy office or a laptop microphone recording in a reverberant room comes out sounding clean, intimate, and broadcast-quality. Podcasters, YouTubers, remote workers recording screencasts, and educators creating course content all use Enhance Speech to elevate the production value of their recordings without investing in acoustic treatment or professional microphones.

Adobe Podcast is free with an Adobe account, with no subscription required for the Enhance Speech feature. The Mic Check feature is a unique addition: before recording, it analyzes your microphone setup in real time and tells you exactly how to improve positioning, distance, and background noise for the best possible source recording. The platform's limitation is scope — it is purely an audio cleaning tool with no recording, editing, chapter marking, or distribution features. Users who need a complete podcast production suite should combine it with Descript or Audacity.

Advantages
  • Completely free audio enhancement — no subscription required
  • Mic Check feature guides microphone placement before you record
  • Removes background noise, echo and room reverb from any audio source in seconds
Disadvantages
  • Enhancement only — no recording, editing or podcast publishing features
  • Can introduce occasional audio artifacts on heavily degraded source recordings
