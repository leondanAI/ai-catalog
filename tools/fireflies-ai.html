About Fireflies.ai

Fireflies.ai is an AI meeting assistant that automatically joins video calls, records, transcribes, and analyzes meetings across Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, and other platforms. It reached 20 million users and achieved a $1 billion valuation in June 2025, making it the most widely adopted AI meeting tool for small and mid-sized teams.

After each meeting, Fireflies generates a searchable transcript, an AI summary with key topics, and an extracted list of action items with assigned owners. The AI Insights feature tracks speaking time, sentiment, filler words, and talk-to-listen ratios — useful for sales coaching and meeting effectiveness review. The search functionality allows querying across all past meeting transcripts for specific topics, decisions, or mentions.

Key capabilities include automatic meeting recording and transcription, AI-generated summaries and action items, speaker diarization (who said what), topic tracking across meetings, AI Insights for coaching analytics, transcript search, integrations with Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, Notion, and Zapier, and an API for building on meeting data.

Pricing: Free plan provides 800 minutes of meeting storage and 3 months of transcript access. Pro at $10 per user per month provides unlimited storage, transcription, and basic AI features. Business at $19 per user per month adds AI Insights analytics, CRM integrations, and team management. Enterprise at $39 per user per month adds SSO, compliance features, and dedicated support.

Limitations: AI summary quality varies by meeting type — structured meetings produce better summaries than unstructured brainstorms. The bot joining meetings is visible to participants and may feel intrusive in sensitive discussions. Action item extraction requires clear verbal commitments — implied or indirect action items are often missed.

Best suited for sales teams, customer success managers, and distributed teams that hold frequent external or internal meetings and want automated notes, follow-ups, and a searchable archive of all conversations.

Advantages
  • Automatically joins calls across Zoom, Meet, Teams, and Webex without manual setup
  • AI Insights provides coaching analytics — speaking time, sentiment, talk-to-listen ratio
  • Searchable transcript archive across all past meetings
  • Integrates with Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, Notion, and Zapier
  • $1B valuation and 20M users — proven enterprise-grade reliability
Disadvantages
  • Meeting bot visible to participants — intrusive in sensitive or confidential discussions
  • Action item extraction misses indirect or implied commitments
  • AI summary quality inconsistent for unstructured or creative brainstorm sessions
  • Business tier ($19/user/mo) required for CRM integrations and AI coaching analytics

Choose Fireflies.ai if…

  • ✅ You want automatic meeting recording, transcription, and AI summary without joining the call
  • ✅ You need CRM integration — Fireflies can automatically log meeting notes to Salesforce or HubSpot
  • ✅ You track conversation intelligence: talk time, sentiment, topic tracking across many meetings
  • ✅ You need meeting analytics across your entire team for coaching and deal intelligence

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Otter.ai used for?
Otter.ai transcribes meetings in real-time and creates searchable notes. It joins Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams automatically or records in-person conversations. It's used for meeting notes, journalism interviews, lecture notes, and any situation requiring accurate transcription.
Is Otter.ai free?
Otter.ai has a free plan with 300 minutes of transcription per month and up to 3 audio imports. Pro is $16.99/month for 1,200 minutes. Business is $30/user/month for team features and advanced integrations.
What is Fireflies.ai?
Fireflies.ai is a meeting intelligence platform that automatically records, transcribes, and summarizes meetings. It provides searchable transcripts, AI meeting summaries, and conversation analytics. It integrates with 50+ tools including Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, and Notion.
Is Fireflies.ai free?
Fireflies.ai has a free plan with 800 minutes of storage per month and basic AI summaries. Pro is $18/user/month for unlimited storage, full AI features, and integrations. Business is $29/user/month for CRM integrations and advanced analytics.
