About Gamma

Gamma is an AI presentation and document creation platform that generates structured slides, documents, and web pages from text prompts. It is used by marketers, founders, educators, and consultants who need professionally designed visual content without slide-by-slide manual formatting. Gamma handles layout, design, and image placement automatically from the initial prompt.

The Generate API reached general availability in January 2026, enabling programmatic content creation at scale. Developers and marketing platforms can now call the Gamma API to generate presentations, documents, or pages automatically — for example, generating a customized deck for each sales prospect or producing a report template for each client without human intervention per output.

Gamma Imagine, launched in March 2026, is a new standalone AI image generation product targeting marketing asset creation. It produces charts, infographics, social graphics, and promotional visuals directly within the Gamma platform. This positions Gamma as a direct competitor to Canva's AI image features, with a particular focus on data visualization and structured marketing graphics rather than freeform artistic generation.

A new Ultra tier was introduced alongside Gamma Imagine, offering higher generation limits, priority processing, and access to the image generation capabilities. The platform achieved SOC 2 Type II certification, making it suitable for enterprise teams with compliance requirements around data security and vendor trust.

Pricing in 2026: Free plan at 400 credits, Plus at $8/month annual, Pro at $15/month annual, and Ultra at custom or higher-tier pricing. The free tier is generous for individual users who need occasional decks.

Gamma is best for marketers, salespeople, and founders who need AI-generated presentations and marketing graphics quickly, and for development teams that want to automate presentation generation via API.

Advantages
  • Generate API now GA — programmatic content creation at scale for any platform
  • Gamma Imagine: AI image generation for charts, infographics, and social graphics built in
  • SOC 2 Type II certified — suitable for enterprise teams with compliance requirements
  • Free tier includes 400 credits — sufficient for occasional individual use
  • Handles layout and design automatically — no slide-by-slide formatting required
Disadvantages
  • Ultra tier pricing not publicly disclosed — requires inquiry for enterprise plans
  • Gamma Imagine image quality is optimized for structured graphics, not artistic generation
  • API access requires developer setup — not self-serve for non-technical users
  • Free plan credits are consumed quickly when generating multiple full presentations

Choose Gamma if…

  • ✅ You want to generate a polished presentation from a text prompt in under 2 minutes
  • ✅ You prefer card-based, web-native presentations that are shareable as links — no downloads needed
  • ✅ You want AI to write the content and design the slides simultaneously
  • ✅ You need to embed video, analytics on who viewed your deck, and interactive elements

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Gamma AI?
Gamma is an AI presentation tool that generates complete, designed presentations from a text prompt. Unlike traditional slide builders, Gamma creates card-based web presentations that are shared as links. It generates content, layout, and visuals simultaneously in seconds.
Is Gamma free?
Gamma has a free plan with 400 AI credits (enough for several full presentations). Plus is $10/month and Pro is $20/month for unlimited AI generation, custom domains, and analytics on who views your decks.
Can Beautiful.ai export to PowerPoint?
Yes. Beautiful.ai can export presentations to PowerPoint format (.pptx). This makes it useful when you need to share files with people who expect traditional slide formats. Gamma's web-native format doesn't export to PowerPoint as cleanly.
Which is better for business presentations — Gamma or Beautiful.ai?
For fast AI-generated presentations shared digitally, Gamma is faster and more modern. For traditional business presentations that may need PowerPoint export or a conventional slide-by-slide structure, Beautiful.ai gives more control. Both produce professional results.
