About SurferSEO

SurferSEO is a content optimization platform that scores your article against the actual top-ranking pages on Google for your target keyword, telling you in real time what to add, remove, or adjust to improve your chances of ranking. It does this by analyzing the content of the top 10-20 ranking pages and extracting the key topics, semantic terms, word counts, and structural patterns they share.

The Content Editor is the main product: write or paste your article, enter your target keyword, and SurferSEO shows you a content score (0-100) alongside a list of suggested terms and phrases to include, derived from the top-ranking competitors. The score updates as you write, giving you immediate feedback on whether adding a section or paragraph improves your content's topical coverage. The tool integrates directly with Google Docs and has a WordPress plugin for in-place optimization.

SurferSEO is a grading and optimization tool, not a content strategy tool — it tells you how to rank better for keywords you have already chosen, but does not help with keyword research, backlink building, or technical SEO. For a complete SEO workflow, most teams pair it with Semrush or Ahrefs for research and Surfer for content optimization. The Starter plan is $79/month, making it most cost-effective for teams publishing at least 10-15 optimized articles per month.

Advantages
  • Real-time content score vs actual top 10 Google results as you write
  • Keyword density and NLP term suggestions backed by SERP data
  • Integrates with Google Docs and WordPress for in-place scoring
Disadvantages
  • Grading tool only — does not replace broader SEO strategy
  • Starts at $79/month
