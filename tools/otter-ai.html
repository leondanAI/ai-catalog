About Otter.ai

Otter.ai started life as a real-time transcription service for meetings and interviews, turning spoken conversations into searchable, shareable notes across Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. That capability is still at the core, and for many people it remains the main reason to use the product.

The big shift came in April 2026, when Otter announced the Otter Meeting Agent and rebranded the whole product as a Conversational Knowledge Engine. Instead of quietly transcribing in the background, Otter now fields voice-activated AI agents: the Meeting Agent joins calls, answers questions, and completes tasks using a company's accumulated meeting data; the Sales Agent offers real-time coaching and objection handling on live calls; and the SDR Agent is a multimodal agent that can autonomously run product demos.

Alongside the agents, Otter rebuilt AI Chat into a private-by-default assistant with a ChatGPT/Claude-style interface, and turned Otter into both an MCP client and server so it can pull context from Gmail, Google Drive, Notion, and Salesforce. Otter for Desktop on Mac and Windows now captures audio and video from any app, not just supported video-conferencing tools. The company says it crossed $100M in annual recurring revenue.

Pricing stays freemium. Basic is free with 300 transcription minutes per user each month, a 30-minute cap per conversation, and 3 lifetime file imports. Pro is $16.99 per user monthly or $8.33 billed annually ($100/year), lifting you to 1,200 minutes a month, 90 minutes per conversation, and 10 file imports monthly. Business is $30 per user monthly or $19.99 billed annually, with unlimited meeting and in-app recording transcription, 4 hours per conversation, and unlimited imports. Enterprise is custom-priced and adds SSO, SCIM, an optional HIPAA add-on, and API access. Students with a .edu address get 20% off Pro.

The limitations are worth knowing. The free tier's minute and import caps are tight for regular use, the most compelling agent features sit behind Business and Enterprise, and letting an AI agent actively participate in meetings raises real privacy and accuracy questions that not every team or client will accept. Transcription quality also still varies with accents, audio quality, and crosstalk.

Otter suits sales teams, customer-facing organizations, and knowledge workers who run many meetings and want an assistant that not only records but retrieves and acts on that history. If you only need occasional, straightforward transcription, the free or Pro tier covers it without touching the agent layer.

Advantages
  • Strong, well-established transcription with live captions across Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams
  • New AI agents (Meeting, Sales, SDR) can answer questions, coach on calls, and even run demos
  • Rebuilt AI Chat is private-by-default and pulls context from Gmail, Google Drive, Notion, and Salesforce via MCP
  • Desktop app for Mac and Windows captures audio and video from any application, not just video-call tools
  • Genuinely free tier plus a 20% student discount make it easy to try
Disadvantages
  • Free plan is limited to 300 minutes a month, 30 minutes per conversation, and just 3 lifetime file imports
  • The headline agent capabilities are gated to the pricier Business and Enterprise plans
  • An AI agent actively joining meetings raises privacy and consent concerns for some teams and clients
  • Transcription accuracy still degrades with heavy accents, poor audio, or people talking over each other
  • The rapid pivot to an agentic platform adds complexity over what used to be a simple notetaker

Choose Otter.ai if…

  • You run frequent sales or customer calls and want live coaching plus searchable meeting history
  • You want an assistant that acts on past meetings, not just transcribes them
  • You use Gmail, Google Drive, Notion, or Salesforce and want that context inside your notetaker
  • You need to capture audio and video from any desktop app, not only video conferencing
  • You want to start free and only pay once you hit real usage

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Otter.ai used for?
Otter.ai transcribes meetings in real-time and creates searchable notes. It joins Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams automatically or records in-person conversations. It's used for meeting notes, journalism interviews, lecture notes, and any situation requiring accurate transcription.
Is Otter.ai free?
Otter.ai has a free plan with 300 minutes of transcription per month and up to 3 audio imports. Pro is $16.99/month for 1,200 minutes. Business is $30/user/month for team features and advanced integrations.
What is Fireflies.ai?
Fireflies.ai is a meeting intelligence platform that automatically records, transcribes, and summarizes meetings. It provides searchable transcripts, AI meeting summaries, and conversation analytics. It integrates with 50+ tools including Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, and Notion.
Is Fireflies.ai free?
Fireflies.ai has a free plan with 800 minutes of storage per month and basic AI summaries. Pro is $18/user/month for unlimited storage, full AI features, and integrations. Business is $29/user/month for CRM integrations and advanced analytics.
