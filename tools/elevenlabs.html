About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is a voice-focused AI company best known for realistic text-to-speech and voice cloning. Beyond TTS it now spans audiobooks, music generation and voice agents, making it one of the more complete audio stacks available to developers and creators.

The current model is Eleven v3, which hit general availability in February 2026. Compared with the older Multilingual v2, v3 substantially expanded language coverage and added the Eleven v3 Conversational model built for voice agents, which can clone a voice from just a few seconds of audio. Around the same time the company launched ElevenMusic with a Music Marketplace for creator monetization, and ElevenReader, an audiobook product at $11/mo positioned against Audible.

Core capabilities include high-quality multilingual TTS, instant and professional voice cloning, real-time conversational agents, music generation and an API with its own subscription tiers for production use.

Pricing runs from Free ($0/mo, 10k credits, roughly 10 minutes of TTS, no commercial rights and required attribution) up through Starter ($6/mo, commercial rights and instant cloning), Creator ($22/mo, professional cloning and 192kbps, often promoted at $11 for the first month), Pro ($99/mo), Scale ($299/mo, 3 seats), Business ($990/mo, 10 seats) and custom Enterprise. Annual billing gives two months free.

The main limitations are credit-based usage that can run out quickly on higher-volume work, no commercial rights or required attribution on the free plan, and costs that climb fast once you need many hours of audio or several seats. Voice cloning also raises consent and misuse concerns you have to manage yourself.

ElevenLabs suits creators, studios and developers who need natural-sounding voices in many languages, teams building voice agents, and anyone producing audiobooks or narration at scale. The company reached roughly $500M ARR by April 2026 and raised a $500M Series D at an $11B valuation, with a secondary sale in July 2026 reportedly targeting around $22B.

Advantages
  • Eleven v3 delivers highly natural, expressive speech across a wide and recently expanded set of languages
  • Voice cloning ranges from instant (a few seconds of audio) to studio-grade professional cloning
  • Conversational v3 model enables real-time voice agents, not just prerecorded TTS
  • Broad product suite: TTS, music (ElevenMusic + Marketplace) and audiobooks (ElevenReader) under one roof
  • Solid API with dedicated subscription tiers for developers
Disadvantages
  • Credit-based pricing can be consumed quickly, so heavy audio work gets expensive fast
  • Free plan has no commercial rights and requires attribution
  • Higher seat counts and long-form audio push you into the pricier Scale/Business tiers
  • Realistic voice cloning carries consent and misuse risks the user must manage
  • The $11 Creator promo is first-month only, then reverts to $22/mo

Choose ElevenLabs if…

  • You need natural multilingual TTS or narration for content at scale
  • You want to build real-time voice agents with the Conversational v3 model
  • You need instant or professional voice cloning
  • You are producing audiobooks or music and want it in one platform
  • You are a developer who wants a mature voice API

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ElevenLabs better than Murf?
ElevenLabs produces higher quality, more natural-sounding voices and has superior voice cloning. Murf is better for e-learning and presentation voiceovers with its integrated video sync and team features. Choose ElevenLabs for quality; Murf for e-learning workflows.
Can ElevenLabs clone my voice?
Yes. ElevenLabs can clone your voice from as little as 1 minute of clean audio. Professional voice cloning (Instant Voice Clone) is available on the Starter plan ($5/month). High-quality cloning (Professional Voice Clone) requires the Creator plan ($22/month).
How much does Murf AI cost?
Murf offers a free plan with 10 minutes of voice generation. Creator plan is $29/month (2 hours/month), Business is $99/month (8 hours/month), and Enterprise is custom.
What languages does ElevenLabs support?
ElevenLabs supports 32+ languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, and more. Voice quality varies by language — English and major European languages have the most natural output.
