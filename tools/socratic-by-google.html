Step-by-step math problem solving from camera or typed input

Photomath is a Google-owned math app that scans handwritten or printed problems with your phone camera and shows step-by-step solutions. It has no formally published version number, and support is now hosted on Google's support site.

Khan Academy's AI tutor, built on OpenAI's models via Microsoft's Azure OpenAI Service with Google Gemini powering some newer features.
