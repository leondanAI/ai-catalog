Frequently Asked Questions

What is Consensus AI?
Consensus is an AI-powered academic search engine that searches 200M+ peer-reviewed papers. Its Deep Search and Research Agent extract key findings, show consensus percentages across studies, and provide cited answers — specifically for scientific and medical questions.
Is Consensus free?
Yes. The free plan includes basic Quick Search, abstract summaries, limited Pro messages, and about 3 Deep Searches per month. Consensus Pro is $10/month, and students can get Pro for $9/month with .edu/.ac verification.
Is Perplexity good for academic research?
Perplexity searches the web including academic sources, but it doesn't specialize in peer-reviewed papers the way Consensus does. For academic research requiring study citations and evidence quality, Consensus is more reliable. Perplexity is better for broad, current-events research and agentic Deep Research.
How much does Perplexity cost?
Perplexity Free is $0 with about 5 Pro searches per day. Perplexity Pro is $20/month or $200/year and includes around 20 Deep Research runs per day; Max is $200/month or $2,000/year. Deep Research runs on Claude Opus 4.5/4.6 for Pro and Max.
