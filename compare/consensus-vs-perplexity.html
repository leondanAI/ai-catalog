Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose Consensus if…
  • ✅ You need answers grounded specifically in peer-reviewed academic and scientific literature rather than general web sources.
  • ✅ You want Deep Search to break a question into sub-questions and synthesize findings across dozens, or up to roughly 1,000, papers.
  • ✅ You want to use AI-powered literature search inside ChatGPT, Claude, or Microsoft 365 Copilot via Consensus Everywhere.
  • ✅ You're tackling a research project and want tools like Skills and a Research Gaps Matrix built for scientific workflows.
Choose Perplexity if…
  • ✅ You want an agentic Deep Research mode that asks clarifying questions, takes mid-run follow-ups, and shows live source tracking.
  • ✅ You need answers from across the broader web, not just indexed academic literature.
  • ✅ You want to compare answers from multiple AI models, like those from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google, side by side with Model Council.
  • ✅ You want an AI-assisted browsing experience through the Comet browser alongside your search.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Consensus?
Consensus is an AI research engine that searches peer-reviewed academic and scientific literature to answer questions with cited evidence, and has expanded into an AI workspace with features like Skills and a Research Gaps Matrix.
Is Consensus free?
Consensus offers a freemium plan, though the exact free-tier usage limits are not published. Paid tiers are Pro and a higher Deep plan, which include a set number of Deep Searches per month.
Which is better for academic research, Consensus or Perplexity?
Consensus is purpose-built for peer-reviewed scientific literature, with Deep Search synthesizing across many papers and linking directly to sources. Perplexity is a broader answer engine with agentic Deep Research and multi-model access, better suited to general or current-events research than literature-specific work.
How much does Perplexity cost?
Perplexity's Free tier costs $0 a month. Pro is listed at $17 a month billed annually, and Max is listed at $167 a month billed annually; a separate monthly-billing price is not published on the site.
