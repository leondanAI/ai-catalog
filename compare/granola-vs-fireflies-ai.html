Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose Granola if…
  • ✅ You want meeting notes without a bot joining the call, since Granola captures audio directly from your own device.
  • ✅ You like typing rough notes during a meeting and having AI merge them with the transcript instead of just getting a generic summary.
  • ✅ You need an app that follows you everywhere, since Granola runs on Mac, Windows, iPhone, Android and Apple Watch.
  • ✅ You use Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor and want your meeting notes available inside those tools through Granola's MCP connector.
Choose Fireflies.ai if…
  • ✅ You want a bot that automatically joins and records your Zoom, Google Meet or Teams calls without you starting a capture yourself.
  • ✅ You need to ask natural-language questions about past meetings, which Fireflies.ai's AskFred assistant is built for.
  • ✅ You want free, unlimited system-wide voice dictation in 90+ languages, which Fireflies Talk offers on every plan including Free.
  • ✅ You run a sales or customer-facing team and want automations that autofill CRM fields or post updates to Slack.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better for someone who doesn't want a bot visibly joining their calls?
Granola is the better fit since it records from your own device's audio rather than sending a bot into the meeting. Fireflies.ai's core workflow relies on a bot joining the call to record and transcribe.
Which is better for sales teams that live in their CRM?
Fireflies.ai is built for this, with integrations that autofill CRM fields and post updates to Slack automatically. Granola connects to tools like HubSpot and Attio too, but its CRM-style automations are less central to its design.
Does either tool offer voice dictation outside of meetings?
Fireflies.ai does, through Fireflies Talk, a free system-wide dictation feature for Mac and Windows available on every plan including Free. Granola does not offer this; it focuses on meeting notes.
Which tool works on more devices?
Granola covers more ground with apps for Mac, Windows, iPhone, Android and Apple Watch. Fireflies.ai's dictation feature is limited to Mac and Windows desktop apps.
Which is better for using meeting notes inside AI tools like Claude or ChatGPT?
Granola has an edge here with its MCP connector, which brings meeting notes directly into Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor. Fireflies.ai's AskFred lets you query meetings, but it does not offer the same direct MCP integration described in its card.
