Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose Google Stitch if…
  • ✅ You want to try AI UI generation without committing to any paid tier, since Google Stitch launched as a free Google Labs experiment with no paid tier.
  • ✅ You want to turn a spoken idea, a screenshot, an existing codebase or a design file into a UI, since Stitch accepts voice, text, code and design-file inputs alongside the conversational Stitch Agent.
  • ✅ You want to explore many layout directions at once on an infinite canvas before committing to one design.
  • ✅ You want to hand off results through multiple channels, since Stitch exports to Figma, front-end code, Google Antigravity, Netlify publishing and shareable Google AI Studio links.
Choose Figma AI if…
  • ✅ You want AI output to land directly inside your existing design files, since Figma AI is built into the Figma editor rather than requiring export/import.
  • ✅ You want to go from a prompt to a working interactive prototype with a real backend, since Figma Make can connect to Supabase to build functional apps.
  • ✅ You need more than UI layout generation, since Figma AI also covers image generation and editing, rewriting, translation, and AI workflows for imagery, video and audio via the Weave suite.
  • ✅ You want AI agents to work directly with your design files, since Figma AI includes an MCP server and connectors for that purpose.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Google Stitch?
Google Stitch is a free Google Labs experiment that turns text, voice or image prompts into UI designs and front-end code on an infinite canvas. A May 2026 update added real-time canvas streaming, a conversational Stitch Agent, and new export options including Figma, front-end code, Google Antigravity, Netlify publishing and Google AI Studio links.
What is Figma AI?
Figma AI is the set of generative features built into the Figma editor, including Figma Make for prompt-to-prototype workflows, First Draft, AI image generation and editing, and rewriting and translation tools, all metered through a shared AI-credit system across plans.
Which is better for building a working, interactive prototype?
Figma AI has the edge here, since Figma Make can turn a prompt into an interactive prototype and even connect to a Supabase backend for functional apps. Google Stitch focuses on generating UI screens and code rather than wiring up a backend.
Which is better if I just want to try AI UI design for free?
Google Stitch is the simpler free option, since it launched with no paid tier and no published usage limits. Figma AI also offers a free Starter plan with monthly AI credits, but it is capped at 150 credits per day and 500 per month.
Do I need a Figma account to use either tool?
Google Stitch is a standalone Google Labs tool and does not require Figma, though it can export designs to Figma. Figma AI is a layer of features inside Figma itself, so you need a Figma account and plan to use it.
Which tool gives more control over components and design systems?
Neither card confirms dedicated component-library or design-system features for Stitch, while Figma AI operates inside the full Figma editor, which is built around reusable components and files. For that reason Figma AI is the stronger choice if component control matters to you.
