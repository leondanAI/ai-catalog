It runs its own Eleven v4 and Eleven v4 Turbo voice models and aggregates third-party models from ByteDance, Google, OpenAI, Black Forest Labs and others, so one subscription covers most media formats.AI voice platform built around a studio-style voiceover editor (200+ voices, 30+ languages and accents) for e-learning, explainer videos, and presentations, alongside a separate conversational voice-agent product and a low-latency text-to-speech API (Falcon).

Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose ElevenLabs if…
  • ✅ You want to clone a voice from about 10 seconds of audio, with an option to upgrade to studio-grade professional cloning.
  • ✅ You're building a real-time voice agent and want to choose the reasoning model from OpenAI, Anthropic or Google, or connect your own LLM endpoint.
  • ✅ You need one credit balance that covers voice, music, sound effects, image and video generation with lip sync instead of juggling separate subscriptions.
  • ✅ You need natural, expressive speech across more than 90 languages for narration or audiobook-style content.
Choose Murf AI if…
  • ✅ You're producing e-learning courses or explainer videos and need a built-in editor that syncs voiceover to slides and video.
  • ✅ You want a large, ready-made voice library with 200+ voices across 30+ languages and accents, plus style, pitch and emphasis controls.
  • ✅ You're building a real-time conversational app and want the Falcon-based API advertising sub-100ms time-to-first-audio.
  • ✅ You want a Studio plan with commercial rights and full access to the voice library for ongoing production work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better for e-learning and explainer videos?
Murf AI is the stronger fit because Studio includes a built-in editor that syncs voiceover to slides and video, along with style, pitch and emphasis controls built for presentations. ElevenLabs can generate the narration but doesn't offer that same video-sync editor.
Can ElevenLabs clone my voice?
Yes. ElevenLabs offers instant voice cloning from about 10 seconds of audio, plus a professional studio-grade cloning option for higher quality; cloning raises consent and misuse concerns you need to manage yourself.
How does Murf AI's Studio pricing work?
Studio has a free plan with 10 projects and 10 minutes of voice generation per month with no downloads or commercial rights, a Creator plan at $19/month billed annually with an annual pool of 24 hours of voice generation and commercial rights, and a Business plan at $66/month billed annually with 96 hours per year but no voice cloning and only one editor seat; Enterprise is custom-priced.
Does ElevenLabs do more than text-to-speech?
Yes. Beyond TTS and voice cloning, ElevenLabs covers music generation, sound effects, image and video generation with lip sync, and real-time conversational voice agents, all drawn from a single credit balance.
