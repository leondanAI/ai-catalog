Whisper vs Otter.ai

Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose Whisper if…
  • ✅ You want a free, open-source speech-to-text model you can self-host under the MIT license with no usage fees.
  • ✅ You need broad multilingual transcription, since Whisper's large-v3 models cover roughly 99 languages and the non-turbo checkpoints can translate speech into English.
  • ✅ You want to run transcription on your own hardware for privacy, offline use, or batch processing with large-v3-turbo's faster speed.
  • ✅ You're building your own application and want to choose between free open weights or OpenAI's pay-per-minute hosted API, like gpt-transcribe or gpt-live-transcribe, instead of a per-seat subscription.
Choose Otter.ai if…
  • ✅ You run frequent video calls on Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams and want live transcription and captions built in without setup.
  • ✅ You want AI meeting agents that can answer questions, coach sales calls, or run product demos using your team's accumulated meeting history.
  • ✅ You want a ready-to-use desktop app for Mac or Windows that captures audio and video from any application, not just video-conferencing tools.
  • ✅ You need CRM-style workflows, since Pro and above connect to Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zapier, with deeper AI Chat integrations on Enterprise.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is OpenAI Whisper?
Whisper is OpenAI's open-source speech recognition model, released under the MIT license so it can be downloaded and run on your own hardware. The current open checkpoint, large-v3-turbo, keeps most of the accuracy of the full large-v3 model while running about 8x faster, and the family covers roughly 99 languages.
Is Whisper free?
The open-source Whisper models are free to download and self-host; you only pay for your own compute. OpenAI also offers hosted transcription through its API, billed per minute through models like gpt-transcribe and gpt-live-transcribe, rather than being free.
Which is better for team meetings — Whisper or Otter.ai?
Otter.ai is purpose-built for meetings, with live transcription across Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams plus AI agents that can join calls and draw on meeting history. Whisper is a general-purpose transcription model without meeting-specific features like calendar integration, so it suits developers building a custom pipeline more than teams wanting an out-of-the-box meeting assistant.
Which is more accurate — Whisper or Otter.ai?
Neither card gives a direct head-to-head accuracy figure: Whisper's accuracy is strongest for high-resource languages like English and drops for less common ones, while Otter.ai's transcription quality is described as varying with accents, audio quality, and crosstalk. Your choice likely depends more on whether you need multilingual coverage versus meeting-specific features than on a single accuracy number.
