Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose Descript if…
  • ✅ You want to edit podcasts or videos by editing a transcript instead of dragging clips on a timeline.
  • ✅ You want an AI co-editor that drafts scripts, writes show notes, and automatically finds shareable clips for you.
  • ✅ You need audio editing, video editing, and screen recording combined in a single app.
  • ✅ You want to pick between different AI models from Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI to control how your edits and generations turn out.
Choose Adobe Podcast if…
  • ✅ You just need to clean up noisy or echoey voice recordings and make them sound studio-quality.
  • ✅ You want to separate speech, background noise, and music into independent stems you can control or export.
  • ✅ You only produce occasional short audio clips and want a free tool without committing to a subscription.
  • ✅ You want a lightweight browser-based tool with no app to install and no heavy local processing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Descript used for?
Descript is a transcript-based audio and video editor where editing the text edits the recording. It now centers on Underlord, an AI co-editor that drafts scripts, writes show notes, finds shareable clips, and can generate or extend video.
Is Adobe Podcast free?
There is a free tier, but it is audio-only with tight limits on file size, daily enhancement time, and downloads. Fuller features, including video support and higher limits, require the paid Adobe Firefly Pro plan.
Which is better for cleaning up noisy podcast audio?
Adobe Podcast is built specifically around this with its Enhance Speech feature and source separation for speech, noise, and music. Descript also includes AI-based noise and filler-word cleanup, but it's one part of a broader editing workflow rather than the main focus.
Does Descript have AI voice cloning?
Descript lists voice cloning among its capabilities alongside AI video editing and dubbing. It generates AI voices as part of its broader AI editing toolkit rather than as a standalone feature.
