Granola vs Otter.ai

OverviewGranola is an AI notepad that transcribes meetings from your device's audio without a bot joining the call, then combines your rough notes and the transcript into structured notes, action items and follow-ups. Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose Granola if…
  • ✅ You want to keep typing your own rough notes during a call and have AI merge them with the transcript instead of handing you a generic summary.
  • ✅ You don't want a bot joining your call and prefer audio captured straight from your own device across Zoom, Meet, Teams, huddles or in-person chats.
  • ✅ You use Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor and want your meeting notes pulled into those tools through Granola's MCP connector and API.
  • ✅ You need meeting notes on an Apple Watch as well as Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android.
Choose Otter.ai if…
  • ✅ You want an AI agent that can actually join meetings, answer questions and act on your accumulated meeting history, not just take notes.
  • ✅ You run live sales calls and want real-time coaching, objection handling or an SDR agent that can run product demos.
  • ✅ You need a desktop app that captures audio and video from any application, not only from supported video-conferencing tools.
  • ✅ You want an established transcription service with a 10M+ user base behind it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better for running meetings without a bot joining the call?
Granola is built for this: it captures audio from your own device across Zoom, Meet, Teams, huddles and in-person conversations, so no bot ever shows up in the call.
Which is better for sales teams that want in-call coaching?
Otter.ai, since its Sales Agent offers real-time coaching and objection handling, and its SDR Agent can run product demos, features Granola does not offer.
Do both tools let AI assistants use my meeting data?
Yes. Granola has an MCP connector and API that bring meeting notes into Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor, while Otter is both an MCP client and server and adds API and webhook access on Enterprise.
Which free plan gives you more to work with?
They limit you differently: Granola's free Basic plan shows only the last 30 days of meeting history, while Otter's free Basic plan caps you at 300 transcription minutes a month, 30 minutes per conversation and 3 lifetime file imports.
Which is better if I need CRM integrations?
Both connect to CRMs, but on different tiers: Granola's $14 Business plan links to Attio, Notion, HubSpot, Affinity and Zapier, while Otter's Pro plan adds Salesforce, HubSpot and Zapier, with deeper Gmail, Drive, Notion and Jira context reserved for Otter Enterprise.
