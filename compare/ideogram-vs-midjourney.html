Frequently Asked Questions

What is Ideogram AI?
Ideogram is an AI image generator specifically optimized for rendering text within images. It excels at creating posters, logos, t-shirt designs, and any image where readable text is part of the visual. The current Ideogram 4.0 (released June 2026) also delivers strong photorealism and design capabilities.
Can Midjourney generate text in images?
Midjourney V7 improved text rendering, but it still struggles with accurate, legible text compared to Ideogram. For images where text accuracy is critical — signs, logos, product labels — Ideogram remains the better choice.
Is Ideogram free?
Ideogram has a free plan ($0) with roughly 10 slow credits per week and public generations. Paid tiers start with Basic at about $8/month (around $7/month billed annually) for priority credits, and Plus at about $20/month (around $15-16/month billed annually).
How much does Midjourney cost?
Midjourney has no free tier. Plans are Basic $10/month, Standard $30/month, Pro $60/month, and Mega $120/month, with higher tiers adding more fast GPU time and concurrency.
