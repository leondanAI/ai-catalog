Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose Google Veo if…
  • ✅ You need dialogue, sound effects and ambient audio generated in the same pass as the video itself.
  • ✅ You want to turn a still image into a short video clip with matching audio automatically generated.
  • ✅ You're building a developer pipeline and want predictable per-second, per-resolution API pricing.
  • ✅ You're already working inside Google Flow, the Gemini app or Vertex AI and want video generation built in.
Choose Runway if…
  • ✅ You want to generate and compare outputs from multiple models like Nano Banana Pro, Kling, Seedance and Lyria without leaving the platform.
  • ✅ You want one platform that handles image, video and native audio generation together.
  • ✅ You want a free plan to try the interface before committing to a paid tier.
  • ✅ You want a clear, published monthly subscription price instead of per-second usage billing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Google Veo?
Google Veo is Google's text- and image-to-video model, currently shipping as Veo 3.1, which generates short clips with synchronized dialogue, sound effects and ambient audio in the same generation pass. It's available through Google Flow, the Gemini app, Vertex AI and the Gemini API.
What is Runway?
Runway is a web-based generative media platform for video, image and audio, with Gen-4.5 as its current flagship video model. Beyond its own models, it also hosts third-party models like Kling, Seedance and Nano Banana Pro so users can compare outputs in one place.
Which is better for generating video with synchronized audio?
Google Veo generates dialogue, sound effects and ambient audio in the same step as the video, which is a core strength of Veo 3.1. Runway's Gen-4 family has also added native audio generation alongside video, so both support audio, but Veo's card emphasizes this as its defining feature.
Which is better for comparing multiple AI video models in one place?
Runway is the better fit here, since it hosts third-party models like Nano Banana Pro, Kling, Seedance, Seed Audio and Lyria alongside its own Gen-4.5 model for side-by-side comparison. Google Veo only offers Google's own Veo and Gemini Omni models.
How is pricing structured for each tool?
Google Veo on the Gemini API charges per second of generated video, varying by resolution and by the Lite, Fast or Standard variant chosen, while consumer access runs through Google AI subscription tiers with unpublished USD pricing. Runway uses a credit system with a free one-time 125-credit plan and paid tiers starting at $12/mo billed annually.
