Data Studio (Looker Studio) vs Tableau AI

Data teams: Data Studio (Looker Studio) vs Tableau AI — which AI handles your datasets better? 2026 breakdown.

Data Studio (Looker Studio)
Best for: Free-to-start, Google-integrated BI dashboards and reports (the product formerly called Looker Studio, renamed back to Data Studio in April 2026)
Tableau AI
Best for: Enterprise BI with AI-powered analytics — Salesforce-integrated
OverviewData Studio (formerly Looker Studio, originally Google Data Studio) is Google's business intelligence tool for building interactive dashboards and reports from more than 1,400 data connectors, with a free self-service tier and an optional paid Pro tier. A natural-language Conversational Analytics preview is now available to all users, while some Gemini-assisted features remain tied to the paid tier.Tableau Agent is Salesforce's generative-AI assistant for Tableau (formerly Einstein Copilot for Tableau), adding conversational, natural-language analytics and agentic authoring across Tableau's editions and Tableau Next.

Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose Data Studio (Looker Studio) if…
  • ✅ You want a genuinely free tier with unlimited reports and data sources, needing only a Google account.
  • ✅ You need natural-language Conversational Analytics without paying extra, since it's now available to every Data Studio user.
  • ✅ You rely heavily on Google data sources like BigQuery, Google Ads, Analytics or Sheets and want native integration plus 1,400+ connectors.
  • ✅ You're a small team or individual who can't justify committing to a quote-only or per-edition enterprise BI budget.
Choose Tableau AI if…
  • ✅ You're already standardized on Tableau or the Salesforce ecosystem and want AI authoring built into tools you already use.
  • ✅ You need the Agent to actively draft calculations, charts and explanations, not just answer questions about existing data.
  • ✅ You want a path into broader agentic analytics through Salesforce's Agentforce 360 Platform and Tableau Next.
  • ✅ You're willing to budget for a specific edition like Tableau Cloud+, Tableau+ Bundle, Tableau Next, or Server Standard/Enterprise to get AI features across Authoring, Prep, Catalog, Dashboards and Pulse.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Data Studio free?
Yes, the core Data Studio product is free with only a Google account required, offering unlimited reports and data sources plus access to over 1,400 connectors. A paid Pro tier exists for team governance, scheduling and some Gemini-assisted features.
How much does Tableau AI cost?
Tableau Agent is not sold as a standalone product; it's bundled into specific editions like Tableau Cloud+ Edition, the Tableau+ Bundle, Tableau Server Standard/Enterprise, or the standalone Tableau Next platform starting at $40/user/month. The most commonly quoted Tableau Cloud Standard and Enterprise tiers do not include it, and the higher-end bundles are quote-only.
Which is better for free natural-language data analysis?
Data Studio, since its Conversational Analytics preview now works in plain language for every user at no cost. Tableau Agent has no free tier and requires a specific paid edition to access natural-language authoring.
What is Tableau AI?
Tableau AI refers to Tableau Agent, Salesforce's generative-AI assistant inside Tableau, formerly called Einstein Copilot for Tableau, which helps build calculations, prep data, suggest visualizations and explain charts through natural language.
