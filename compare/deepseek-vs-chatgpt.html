Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose DeepSeek if…
  • ✅ You want the best free AI for reasoning and math — DeepSeek-V4 delivers frontier-level performance at zero cost on the web
  • ✅ You're a developer needing very cheap API access — deepseek-v4-flash costs about $0.14 per million input tokens, a fraction of GPT-5.5
  • ✅ You work on coding and technical problems that benefit from strong step-by-step reasoning
  • ✅ You want maximum reasoning value for free and don't need image generation or advanced voice
Choose ChatGPT if…
  • ✅ You need built-in image generation, voice conversations, or web browsing in one place
  • ✅ You want the most reliable, well-tested AI with the largest support and plugin ecosystem
  • ✅ You use multimodal tasks: analyzing images, documents, and data with GPT-5.5
  • ✅ You need enterprise features, SOC 2 compliance, and US-based data handling
  • ✅ You want tiered plans — Free, Go at $8, Plus at $20, or Pro at $100-200/mo

Frequently Asked Questions

Is DeepSeek better than ChatGPT?
DeepSeek-V4 matches leading models on reasoning, math, and coding benchmarks at a fraction of the cost, and its web chat is free. ChatGPT (GPT-5.5) offers more built-in features — image generation, voice, browsing — and a larger ecosystem. For pure text reasoning and coding value, DeepSeek is highly competitive; for all-in-one multimodal use, ChatGPT leads.
Is DeepSeek safe to use?
DeepSeek is a Chinese company (Hangzhou DeepSeek AI). Some organizations have data-privacy concerns under Chinese law, and certain US agencies and enterprises restrict its use. The open model weights can be run locally to avoid sending data to DeepSeek's servers.
Is DeepSeek free?
Yes. DeepSeek chat at chat.deepseek.com is free to use in the web interface. The API is also very cheap — deepseek-v4-flash is about $0.14 per million input tokens (cache miss) and $0.28 output, well below ChatGPT's GPT-5.5 pricing.
What is DeepSeek-V4?
DeepSeek-V4 is DeepSeek's current model family, released April 24, 2026, in two variants: V4-Flash (fast and low-cost) and V4-Pro (higher capability). It offers strong reasoning and coding performance while keeping training and inference costs dramatically low.
