Frequently Asked Questions

What is Photomath?
Photomath is a math problem solver app. Point your camera at any problem — handwritten or printed — and it instantly returns step-by-step solutions with explanations, covering arithmetic, algebra, trigonometry, calculus, and statistics. The core app is free (no ads, no time limit), and Photomath Plus adds deeper explanations for $9.99/month or $69.99/year.
Is Photomath cheating?
It depends on how you use it. Using Photomath to check your work or understand where you went wrong is educational; copying answers without following the method is counterproductive. The step-by-step explanations are designed to help you learn, not just supply an answer.
What is Duolingo Max?
Duolingo Max is the highest Duolingo subscription tier ($29.99/month or $167.99/year). It adds AI features powered by OpenAI's GPT-4o, including Video Call with the character Lily and Roleplay for practicing real-life conversations, on top of everything in the Super tier.
Is Duolingo Max worth it versus Duolingo Super?
Duolingo Super ($95.99/year) removes ads and unlocks extra practice. Max ($167.99/year or $29.99/month) adds the GPT-4o Video Call and Roleplay conversation features. If you actively practice speaking with Video Call and Roleplay, Max is worth it; if you mainly do standard exercises, Super is enough.
