Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose Hemingway Editor if…
  • ✅ You write content that needs to be clear, simple, and readable — blogs, emails, landing pages
  • ✅ You want to eliminate adverbs, passive voice, and complex sentences for stronger writing
  • ✅ You want a distraction-free editor focused purely on readability — no grammar nitpicking
  • ✅ You're a writer who already knows grammar and needs structural feedback, not spell checking
  • ✅ You prefer a one-time-purchase desktop app (Hemingway Editor Classic) or a simple no-signup free online editor
Choose Grammarly if…
  • ✅ You want real-time grammar, spelling, and punctuation corrections as you type
  • ✅ You write in many places — the browser extension covers Gmail, Docs, Slack, and every text field
  • ✅ You want AI-powered tone detection, clarity suggestions, and full rewriting assistance
  • ✅ You want generative AI prompts to draft, reply, and rewrite — now part of the Superhuman suite
  • ✅ You need a free tier to start and a clear path to more AI usage as you scale

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Hemingway Editor do?
Hemingway Editor analyzes your writing for readability. It highlights hard-to-read and very-hard-to-read sentences, passive voice, complex phrases, and unnecessary adverbs — color-coded by severity — and assigns a grade-level readability score. The goal is writing that's clear, direct, and easy to read.
Is Hemingway Editor free?
Yes, there's a free online Hemingway Editor at hemingwayapp.com with readability highlighting and no signup required. For more, Hemingway Editor Plus is the paid, AI-powered web plan, and there's also a one-time-purchase desktop app, Hemingway Editor Classic.
Is Grammarly better than Hemingway?
They do different things. Grammarly checks grammar, spelling, and style with real-time suggestions and AI writing, while Hemingway focuses on sentence structure and readability. Many writers use both: Grammarly for correctness, Hemingway for clarity. Grammarly is more comprehensive; Hemingway is more focused.
How much does Grammarly cost?
Grammarly has a Free plan at $0 with 100 AI prompts per month. Pro is about $12 per member/month billed annually (around $30/month monthly) with 2,000 AI prompts per month and a 7-day trial. Enterprise pricing is custom. Grammarly's AI features are now part of the Superhuman suite.
