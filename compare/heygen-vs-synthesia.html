Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between HeyGen and Synthesia?
HeyGen leads on video translation (lip-synced dubbing in 175+ languages) and natural-looking avatar quality. Synthesia leads on enterprise features, compliance, and LMS integration for corporate training. HeyGen targets marketing; Synthesia targets L&D teams.
Can HeyGen clone my voice?
Yes. HeyGen can clone your voice and create an AI avatar that looks and sounds like you from a short recording. This is used for video translation — your avatar speaks in other languages with your cloned voice.
Is Synthesia free?
Synthesia offers a free plan with 3 minutes of video per month. Paid plans start at $29/month for the Starter plan (10 minutes) and $89/month for Creator. Enterprise pricing is custom.
How much does HeyGen cost?
HeyGen's Creator plan starts at $29/month (4 minutes of video). Business is $89/month (15 minutes). Enterprise pricing is custom. Video translation credits are sold separately.
