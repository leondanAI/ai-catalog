The company says it has more than 40 million users and passed a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate in September 2026.Kling AI is a text- and image-to-video generator from Kuaishou. Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose Higgsfield if…
  • ✅ You want to switch between models like Kling, Seedance, Veo and Nano Banana within a single subscription instead of juggling separate accounts.
  • ✅ You need ready-made workflows for ads, product placement or AI influencer content instead of building scenes from scratch.
  • ✅ You run a team and want pooled credits, admin spend controls and custom SSO for shared production work.
  • ✅ You want to try the platform on a free plan before committing to a paid tier.
Choose Kling AI if…
  • ✅ You need a single clip up to 30 seconds in one pass rather than stitching together shorter segments.
  • ✅ You want Omni Reference to keep up to 15 characters, objects or style references consistent across multiple shots.
  • ✅ You write long, detailed prompts and want support for up to roughly 8,000 tokens of scene direction.
  • ✅ You want two-channel stereo audio generated directly alongside the video instead of adding it separately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better for producing ads and social video using several different AI models?
Higgsfield is the better fit, since it bundles Kling, Seedance, Veo, Nano Banana, FLUX and GPT Image alongside its own ad, product placement and AI influencer tools in one subscription.
Which is better for a single long, consistent video shot?
Kling AI is built for this, with single-pass clips up to 30 seconds, up to 10 keyframes and Omni Reference for keeping characters and objects consistent across shots.
Does Higgsfield give you access to Kling's models?
Yes, Higgsfield lists Kling among the third-party models available inside its platform, alongside Seedance, Veo, Nano Banana and others, billed through its own credit system.
Is there a free way to try either tool?
Higgsfield offers a Free plan with limited use, while the Kling AI card does not confirm any free plan and gates Kling 4.0 Flash early access behind an Ultra yearly subscription.
Which is better for team collaboration on video production?
Higgsfield is the clearer choice here, with team plans that include pooled credits, admin spend controls and custom SSO, features the Kling AI card does not describe.
