Verdict: Which Should You Choose?

Choose Duolingo Max if…
  • ✅ You're learning a new language and want AI conversation practice like Video Call with Lily and Roleplay, powered by GPT-4o
  • ✅ You love gamified learning — streaks, XP, leagues — to stay motivated every day
  • ✅ You want realtime spoken practice with an AI character in true-to-life scenarios
  • ✅ You're a casual learner who wants bite-sized lessons on mobile anytime
Choose Khanmigo if…
  • ✅ You're a student needing help with math, science, history, or the humanities
  • ✅ You want a Socratic tutor that guides you to the answer instead of just handing it over
  • ✅ You're a teacher wanting AI help with lesson planning, feedback, and student support — free in 44+ countries thanks to Microsoft
  • ✅ You study with Khan Academy and want AI built into that curriculum

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Duolingo Max?
Duolingo Max is Duolingo's top subscription tier, adding AI features powered by GPT-4o. Its flagship feature is Video Call — a realtime spoken conversation with the AI character Lily — alongside Roleplay, where you practice real-life dialogues with AI characters. It costs $167.99/year or $29.99/month.
What is Khanmigo?
Khanmigo is Khan Academy's AI tutor, built on GPT-4 (with GPT-4o multimodal integration in progress). It helps students across academic subjects using the Socratic method — asking guiding questions rather than giving direct answers — and is tied to Khan Academy's curriculum.
Is Khanmigo free?
Khanmigo is free for teachers in 44+ countries, supported by Microsoft. For learners and parents/families it costs $4/month or $44/year (the annual plan saves roughly two months). A parent account can add up to 10 children.
How much does Duolingo cost?
Duolingo has a free tier at $0. Super is $95.99/year, Duolingo Max is $167.99/year or $29.99/month, and Super Family starts at $119.99/year for up to 6 people. Only Max includes the GPT-4o AI features like Video Call and Roleplay.
